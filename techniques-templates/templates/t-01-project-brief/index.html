01Situation

Every engagement opens the same way: two weeks of interviews, a deck nobody rereads, and a diagnosis that lands after the decision is already made. Teams know something is off — they lack an instrument that shows them where.

The gap is not insight. It is speed, and evidence a team can hold.

02Who it is for

Founders and heads of brand at companies of 50 to 500, mid-pivot. Technical enough to trust a measurement, impatient enough to reject a workshop. They buy instruments, not opinions.

03Mandate

Build a portable diagnostic a team can run on itself in one afternoon and read without us in the room. Not a report — a working instrument: inputs, a visible method, a result they can act on the same day.

04Territories
A · The Bench

Lab equipment. Calibrated, honest, a little clinical. The output is a reading, not a recommendation.

B · The Field Kit

Gear you carry in. Rugged, self-contained, made for the site rather than the boardroom.

C · The Mirror

A surface that shows a company its own structure — no verdict, just an exposed mechanism it cannot unsee.

  • Two of three pilot teams ask to run it again.
