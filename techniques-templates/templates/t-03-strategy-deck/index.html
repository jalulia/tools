/ What the strategy commands

Every word is an instruction—not decoration.

Stop impersonating.

Drop the restaurant fantasy. Name the freezer, the microwave, the process and the actual job without apology.

Start arriving.

Begin with the finished bite and design every handoff so the promise survives contact with real life.

As yourself.

Let every dish keep its coordinates, character and standards while becoming fully at home here.

The COOKT Standard: It is not COOKT until the dish is specific enough to want, substantial enough to resolve the meal, and every meaningful promise can stand up to scrutiny.
The strategy becomes useful when it changes decisions.
