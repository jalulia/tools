01Stage

Everything is composed on a fixed 1600 × 900 stage and scaled to the viewport; when the viewport is wider or taller than 16 : 9 the stage bleeds (--bleed-x, --bleed-y) so full-bleed grounds still fill the screen. Slides pad 58 / 64 / 54 px; covers and section turns pad 70 px on the sides.

1600 × 90058 · 64 · 54 paddingbleed fills the rest
Fitscale(min(vw/1600, vh/900)) with --bleed-x/--bleed-y so full-bleed grounds fill the viewport
Runtime#slide-id or #n deep links; replaceState on navigate
swipe ≥ 55px
print each slide at 1600 × 900 (the source deck was exported to a 41-page PDF this way)

03Type

Poppins 900 carries every display line, uppercase, tracked −.04em at .96 leading. Poppins 300 is the voice, 500 the landing. Inter carries body, labels and meta (the source deck used Proxima Nova; Inter substitutes so the template ships with open fonts).

04Layouts

Each card is one layout, shown as it appears in the worked example, with its usage note. Click any card to open that slide in the deck; duplicate the slide, replace the copy, keep the frame.

01covervoid

Cover. One h1 at 132 px, three to four words, the last one or two in coral. Title line under the rule, date and studio at right. The field art is the ø co. warm/cool plate; the veil is 42 % black.
02layout keyvoid

Layout key. Generated from the slide list at load; delete this slide in a real deck. Keeps the deck honest about what it contains.
03section turnvoid

Section turn. Code in coral, one h1 at 143 px, bottom-left. Two to four words. The deep veil (58 %) keeps the type legible on any part of the field.
04copy ledgervoid

Copy + ledger, 54/46. Left: label, h2 at 72 px, two short paragraphs. Right: three to five rows, two-digit number in coral at 68 px, one line each. Numbers should be real counts, not decoration.
05image splitvoid

Image split, 58/42, photo left. Label, h2 at 70 px, one voice line (Poppins 300, 30 px). Photo is object-fit cover with a 10 % black wash; put the subject in the center 60 %. Variants of this family: product-split 53/47 (copy left, light), story-split 57/43 (adds a green landing line), aisle-split 62/38, means-split 41/59 (h2 at 91 px).
06stat rowvoid

Stat row. Four stats on hairlines, number in green at 92 px, one sentence each (≤ 270 px wide). Landing line pinned to the bottom rule. Use three or four stats; two reads thin, five overflows.
07tradeoffvoid

Trade-off. Two panels on hairlines with a green VS between; the right panel is the risk and fills coral. Each panel: a mono heading, one Poppins 500 line at 39 px, a dotted list. Green landing line closes.
08halveslight

Two halves, coral | white. Each half: label, big statement at 88 px (min-height 320 so the pair aligns), one paragraph, a dotted ingredients line. Keep the two statements the same length. The house layout (slide 21) is the same frame in navy | green.
09copy momentsnavy

Copy + moments on navy, 41/59. Three moments (a quoted line in Poppins 900 at 29 px, one sentence). The foot line is optional: an accent lead-in and one sentence pinned to the bottom rule.
10order rowsvoid

Order rows. Two or three rows: a mono key, then a sequence in Poppins 900 at 53 px with arrows. The row that matters carries class retail (coral). One voice line closes.
11big numbervoid

Big number. Copy left (h2 at 84 px, one paragraph), one number in green at 235 px right with a mono caption. The number is the whole slide: two or three characters, no decimals.
12image split landingvoid

Image split with a landing, 57/43. Same as the image split plus a green landing line under the body. Use it when the slide has to hand off to the next idea.
13term pairnavy

Term pair on navy. Two terms on hairlines: first in coral, second in pale blue, Poppins 900 at 77 px (min-height 140 so they align), one paragraph each. The landing spans both columns.
15card gridvoid

Card grid ×6. Six cards at 448 px, image at 388 px on a paper ground (object-fit contain), a mono name strip. Takeaway below: mono key + one Poppins 300 line. The challenger variant is five cards at 400 px with object-fit cover.
16column gridlight

Column grid ×4 on white. Four columns on hairlines: number, one word in Poppins 900 at 48 px (first coral, third blue), one sentence. The last column fills green. The words layout (slide 24) is the three-column version.
17research platevoid

Research plate. One image in a hairline frame at 787 px on the panel ground, two mono captions above. For evidence that should be seen whole: a photo, a screenshot, a source slide.
18statementvoid

Statement. Display at 132 px with the turn in green on its own line, then a voice paragraph at 42 px. The line-statement variant (h1 at 130 px) and the paradox variant (on the field, h1 at 124 px) are the same move with a different ground.
20planevoid

Plane diagram. A Fano plane at 660 × 600: three vertices, three mids, one core, seven labeled points (p1–p7, positions fixed in CSS). Plane fill is the accent (coral; class alt makes it blue and flips the copy to the left with plane-right). Point labels: one mono word in the plane color, then ≤ 6 words.
21bindingvoid

Binding. Two planes (a coral, b blue) at .82 scale joined core-to-core by a green bridge, a label on the bridge, and a heading pair at the top corners (word in the plane color + a mono line). Use after two plane slides.
22image split factsvoid

Image split with facts, 52/48. Photo left; right: label, h2 at 58 px, four to six fact rows (mono key in coral, one line), green landing. The pair-split variant puts two photos side by side at 57 % on white.
23halves flowvoid

Halves with a flow, navy | green. Each half: label, h2 at 64 px (min-height 185), one paragraph, a four-step flow on hairlines. Keep the two flows the same length.
24tracksnavy

Tracks. Two rows × five steps on hairlines: a track name (coral, then pale blue) and five mono-keyed cells. The point is the parallel: step n on both tracks should rhyme. Green landing closes.
25image cardvoid

Image + card. Full-bleed photo with a 34 % scrim, a 700 px card at 86 % black (label, h2 at 71 px, two paragraphs), and a 2 × 2 mono strip bottom-right. Photo should carry detail on the right two-thirds.
27revealvoid

Reveal. On the field: white label, the strategy line at 118 px with the second clause in coral on its own line, one mono line under it. This is the slide the deck exists for; nothing else on it.
28column grid 3light

Column grid ×3 on white with a standard. Three words in Poppins 900 at 48 px (coral, blue, green), one paragraph each, and a black quote bar pinned to the bottom rule for the standard the deck commits to.
29board pairnavy

Board pair on navy, 48/52. Left: h2 at 59 px and a distance board (mono key in coral, a bold lead-in, one sentence). Right: a channel board (name in pale blue Poppins 900, one sentence). Three rows left, four right.
30card rowvoid

Card row ×3 with voice examples. Three panel cards (mono code in coral, name in Poppins 900 at 40 px, one sentence) and three green voice lines on hairlines beneath. Names should sound like campaigns, not categories.
31recaplight

Recap table on white. Key/value rows on hairlines (mono key, Poppins 500 value at 27 px); the last row is the strategy and fills green. Four to six rows.
32sealvoid

Seal. The outward line at 134 px, second clause in green, the ø mark top-right. The last slide; no notes, no logos beyond the mark.

05Image slots

The template ships no photography. Every image position is a slot that draws a seeded field in the palette with a dashed frame and a label, so the layout is judged on the layout. Replace the figure with an image and the layout CSS sizes and crops it.

<figure class="slot" data-slot="Photo · name" data-seed="n" data-tone="dark|light"><canvas></canvas><span class="slot-tag">Photo · name</span></figure>
→ <figure><img src="…" alt="…"></figure> — the layout CSS already sizes and crops it

07Copy rules
  • label: "/ " + a noun phrase, ≤ 6 words
  • h2: one sentence, ≤ 12 words, a period
  • voice: one sentence at 42px, ≤ 24 words
  • landing: one line, green, the close
  • lists: dotted " · " runs inside one line, or rows on hairlines
  • numbers: two digits, real counts
  • one slide, one move
