The convening

Two seed images: liquid crystals between crossed polarizers, blooming teal and orange with a dark cross at every defect; and an optical bench splitting light through a birefringent crystal into ordinary and extraordinary rays. Both point at one unclaimed region — crystal optics — and it fits the archive's existing logic exactly.

Every other specimen imposes a reproduction process — halftone, riso, xerox — that makes matter appear a certain way. Birefringence makes that operation the subject: the interference colours are not in the material, they exist only between crossed polarizers. Remove the analyzer and they vanish. Design of the content, stated as physics — the image is in the crossing, not the crystal. The ray-bench draws the same idea as a diagram: the apparatus itself, on the bench, in the chrome's own line language.

⌁ Creative technologist

Buildable, or it doesn't ship

Everything here reduces to one field and two lookups. One retardance field drives the colour, the extinction, and the cross — one field, many consequences, in a single file.

◇ Domain researcher

A real cause, cited

Colour is a deterministic function of optical path difference (Malus × retardance, integrated over CIE), not a palette. The Michel-Lévy chart is a nomogram, not a gradient. Grounding held for every entry.

◉ Visual cognition

Why it reads as alive

Teal↔orange drive both opponent channels; the Maltese cross is an orientation singularity the eye reads as lawful. Two signatures are non-negotiable: a real defect, and true extinction bands.

❖ Psychoanalysis

What it means to file it

Familiar matter made luminous only by an apparatus; order forming at a defect; a specimen cooling to nothing. The wish to keep the transient — including its own failures.

Analyzer sweeps the dark extinction brushes (they sit where the director aligns with a polarizer axis — the sin²2θ zeros, so a full defect shows four brushes and a half-defect two). Retardance climbs the Michel-Lévy orders — grey, yellow, first-order red, teal, orange — never an arbitrary rainbow. The λ-plate splits every bloom into a teal↔orange complementary pair across the plate's magenta: Γplate ± Γspecimen. That split reveals optical sign; it is not a colour choice.

A · Optics territory

One coherent region expressed across the archive's own index shape: a driver technique, the two atoms it emits, a first-class known-failure, a schematic style, and a time-series coupling.

A1 · techniquecrossed-polariser-interferenceCrossed-polariser interference field
kind technique · lane glsl / canvas2d · status proposed · produces michel-levy-ramp · extinction-brush · couples fbm-noise · oklab-ramp · governed_by composing-computational-material-systems

A retardance field Γ = d·Δn and a director field θ, run through crossed polarizers: I = sin²(2θ)·sin²(πΓ/λ), integrated over the visible band to RGB. One field emits the interference colour, the extinction brushes, and the disclination cross in a single pass.

⌁ Build. Bake a Γ→sRGB Michel-Lévy LUT once, offline; per fragment two lookups — colour from Γ, darkness from sin²2θ. Single file, low cost — shown live above.
◇ Ground. The crystal-optics chain — Malus orientation term × wavelength-selective retardance. Colour is a function of Γ, not a parameter (Sørensen 2013 revised chart).
◉ Perception. Carries a real orientation singularity and true extinction bands; teal↔orange drives both opponent channels, so it stays vivid.
❖ Meaning. Birefringence exists only between crossed polarizers — meaning that appears only when framed. The base atom of an archive that is about framing.
Novelty · no polarization/optics entry exists; distinct from granulation / watercolour-wash — those are pigment causes, this is an optical one.
A2 · atommichel-levy-rampMichel-Lévy retardation ramp
kind atom : colour · lane data / glsl · status proposed · produced-by crossed-polariser-interference · couples oklab-ramp · governed_by composing-computational-material-systems

The physically-derived interference-colour LUT any Γ-like scalar can index — thickness maps, photoelastic stress, soap-film thickness. A reusable colour atom with a provenance.

⌁ Build. A 1024×1 LUT baked by integrating sin²(πΓ/λ) against the CIE curves; ship as an embedded data-URI, sample by Γ/Γmax.
◇ Ground. Newton's colour series; the chart is a nomogram where the diagonals are lines of constant birefringence Δn.
◉ Perception. Lawful, nested, recurring colour order — the structural opposite of a rainbow ramp. This is the anti-rainbow.
❖ Meaning. The physical sibling to the engineered oklab-ramp — the two filed side by side, provenance as content.
Novelty · the only colour atom today is oklab-ramp (perceptual/engineered); this one is physically derived, with a built-in coupling story.
A3 · atomextinction-brushExtinction brush / director field
kind atom : field · lane canvas2d / glsl · status proposed · produced-by crossed-polariser-interference · couples mulberry32 · governed_by composing-computational-material-systems

The sin²(2θ) isogyre field from a director map built of placed disclinations of chosen strength and sign — 4|s| dark brushes per defect, sweeping to reveal the sign.

⌁ Build. θ = Σ sᵢ·atan2(y−yᵢ, x−xᵢ) + θ₀ + coherent noise; darkness = sin²(2(θ−φ)). Ten lines.
◇ Ground. Frank-elastic smooth director between defects; brush count = 4|s| (s=½→2, s=1→4); sweep direction gives sign (Kim et al. 2018).
◉ Perception. The orientation singularity is a high-information anchor; good-continuation binds each brush into one swept stroke.
❖ Meaning. Order forms exactly at the defect — structure organised around a single point.
Novelty · no orientation-defect field exists; fbm-noise is stochastic, this is topological — the archive's first field with a singularity.
A4 · known-failureoptics.extinctionThe black frame
kind known-failure · lane canvas2d / glsl · status proposed · sibling-of unlinked-shader · governed_by composing-computational-material-systems

The interference lens at the extinction angle — the director aligned exactly to the polarizer. Its correct, fully-specified state renders black, save the caption.

⌁ Build. The same engine, φ set to alignment; one parameter, one rule. The finished output is a black rectangle.
◇ Ground. sin²(2θ)=0 at alignment — total extinction is the real optical result of a correctly built instrument, not a bug.
◉ Perception. Absence with a defined cause reads as lawful, not broken — the eye accepts the dark as content.
❖ Meaning. Presence defined by its own disappearance. It files beside "a shader that does not link" and proves the known-failure category is interpretive, not a bug tray.
Novelty · the archive's first known-failure that is a correct physical state rather than an error — the category's honest keystone.

B · Structural gap-fillers

Two techniques chosen not for prettiness but because each closes a hole in the archive's coupling graph — the first sound-to-image cause, and the first evolving field.

B1 · techniquechladni-nodal-fieldChladni / cymatic nodal field
kind technique · lane canvas2d · status proposed · produces nodal-figure · couples banded-burst (audio) · governed_by composing-computational-material-systems · composing-computational-sound-systems

Standing-wave eigenmodes of a driven plate; grains settle where displacement ≈ 0 (the nodal lines). The figure changes discretely as the drive frequency selects a new mode.

⌁ Build. Modal sum W = cos(nπx)cos(mπy) − cos(mπx)cos(nπy); particles gradient-descend |W| onto the zero set. A small frequency→(n,m) table.
◇ Ground. Chladni figures — plate eigenmodes; the drive frequency is the parameter, tying straight into the Sound index.
◉ Perception. Sharp singular nodal loci on a smooth amplitude field — the same smooth-plus-singularity profile that makes birefringence read.
❖ Meaning. The audible made visible; sound leaving a fingerprint on matter.
Novelty · the archive's first true audio↔visual shared-cause coupling — one drive tone (via banded-burst) produces the figure. Fills the gap between the Sound index and the visual spine.
B2 · techniquegray-scott-reaction-diffusionGray-Scott reaction-diffusion
kind technique · lane glsl · status proposed · produces turing-field · couples michel-levy-ramp · bayer8 · governed_by composing-computational-material-systems

A two-chemical PDE; feed F and kill k are the only controls, and the whole Turing zoo — spots, stripes, mitosis, coral — falls out of a two-parameter sweep. The same field drives colour, height, and edge-ink.

⌁ Build. Ping-pong float framebuffers; 9-point Laplacian; ~20 iterations/frame; read V as the atom. Couples cleanly to michel-levy-ramp (V→colour) and bayer8 (dither).
◇ Ground. Turing's 1952 diffusion-driven instability (Pearson 1993); spacing set by the diffusion ratio, an intrinsic wavelength.
◉ Perception. The intrinsic spacing plus real relaxation defects is exactly what reads as alive rather than noise-warped.
❖ Meaning. Global order from local rules — pattern because of an instability, not painted.
Novelty · no PDE / self-organising field exists; fbm-noise is stationary and stochastic — this one evolves and is deterministic. The widest downstream reuse of any candidate.

Acceptance test

The visual-cognition lens left a gate. Any optics field entry must clear it before it graduates from proposed — this is the perceptual QA the Librarian runs on a render, and it slots beside the Lens-Craft standard.

Score each 0–2 · sum capped at 10 · ≥8 authentic · 5–7 salvageable · ≤4 AI gradient
  1. Singularity — at least one well-formed orientation defect with converging brushes; orientation undefined at a defined point.
  2. Extinction structure — near-black isogyre brushes, coincident with orientation, not just chroma.
  3. Lawful colour order — hues nest and recur by an underlying scalar; a complementary pair dominates; no arbitrary rainbow.
  4. Cross-scale coherence — colour, brush, and grain edges register (one field drives all); coherent detail at ≥2 octaves; holds under 4× zoom.
  5. Framing & continuation — bounded circular field with edge response; brushes read as continuous strokes; any labels pass contrast on the brightest bloom and the darkest brush.
Fast-fail · no singularity and no extinction band → cap the total at 3, however beautiful the colour. Those two are the load-bearing signatures of a real optical cause.

The bench — what waits

Three more territories cleared research but were held back so this drop stays coherent. caustic-refraction-web is the strongest next: caustic intensity is 1/|det J| of the refracted ray map, and it supplies the physical cause upstream of the existing edge-bloom atom — it tightens the coupling graph rather than adding a leaf. Behind it, schlieren-shadowgraph (knife-edge ∂n / Laplacian ∇²n — genuinely different physics from LC extinction) and moiré-aliasing (the pattern that makes the act of sampling visible). A candidate governing skill, composing-optical-systems, would hold the physics these share; until then composing-computational-material-systems covers them.
