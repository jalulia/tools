Configuration

Gravity only comes in one sign. Every mass pulls, so all the field lines run inward and every test body falls toward the mass. There is no second sign to make a line point the other way.

No negative mass has ever been found. A +/− pair does something strange on paper: the negative mass is drawn toward the positive, the positive is pushed from the negative, so the pair accelerates off together — a runaway that never settles.
