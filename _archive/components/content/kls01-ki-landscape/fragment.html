Hokkaido, eleven bands

Painted from the back forwards. Each band is a ridge line, a fill, and then seven passes laid one over the other inside that band's own silhouette — never across it.

Three shared causes and seven consequences. Every pass is clipped to the same path, offset by the same drift, and tinted from the same mid colour, so all seven textures travel with the land instead of sitting in front of it. That is the whole difference between a chain and a pile.
