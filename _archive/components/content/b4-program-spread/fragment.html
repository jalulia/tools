OVERTURE GOSPEL MIGRATION

A Musical Family Tree

Conceived by Quincy Sallis, SOUNDTRACK OF AMERICA traces a musical family tree from the field holler to the sampler — five nights of new commissions performed by the artists who carry the line forward. Each program pairs an elder form with the sound it seeded. Doors 7:00, curtain 8:00.
