01 · Thesis & Problem

A persistent shared mind. Your record travels with you across frontier models, and accumulates cleanly without re-reading the whole conversation.

Mindgut separates the model from the memory. The model is the voice in the room; your .helix file is the instrument on the table—keeping what was decided, why it was decided, and what is still open.

Context hostage

Every conversation today lives inside one vendor’s window. Close the tab, switch from Claude to GPT, or hit a project limit, and you are back to pasting summary paragraphs by hand.

Token bloat

To remember, standard chat tools re-read the entire raw transcript on every turn—burning 80 to 90 percent of their window on old chatter until the model slows down and forgets your boundaries.

A portable private interior

Your Helix lives on your disk alongside your local project folders. It stores the relational geometry of your decisions rather than a dead log, so it moves across models and stays light.

Mir is the standing wave that forms between a person, a model, and a kept record. The record is the part you own.
