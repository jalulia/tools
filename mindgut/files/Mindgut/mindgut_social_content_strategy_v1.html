MINDGUT SOCIAL & CONTENT STRATEGY 02 / AGENDA
/ 01 · STRATEGIC POSTURE & GUARDRAILS T05 · TWO-COLUMN SPLIT

Show the effect at 2,000+ strands. Never explain the unfiled engine.

Publicly demonstrating unfiled technical mechanics starts a 1-year US patent clock and can immediately forfeit international patent rights. Every video, caption, and screen capture follows a strict split:

SAFE TO SHOW · THE EFFECT

What Persistent Memory Feels Like in Practice

  • Continuity at scale: Showing real strand depth (Strand 500, Strand 2,140, Strand 7,000+) across weeks of work.
  • Zero re-briefing: Unexpected reach-backs where Mir connects a constraint, decision, or document from weeks ago.
  • Workflow velocity: Plain-English comparisons against stateless chat re-briefs.
  • Visual interface: The workspace field, strand arrivals, and clean domain artifacts.
OFF-LIMITS UNTIL FILED · THE ENGINE

Protected IP & Confidential Data

  • Underlying mathematics: Geometric mechanics, lattice rules, coset partitions, or internal scoring formulas.
  • Scanner & indexing architecture: How memory strands are segmented, weighted, or retrieved under the hood.
  • Unredacted client work: Client names, proprietary formulations, internal financial figures, or personal history.
  • Unregistered marks: Specific coined technical terms not yet cleared for word-mark registration.

MINDGUT SOCIAL & CONTENT STRATEGY 04 / CHANNEL ROLLOUT
/ 03 · CONTENT PILLARS & PRIVACY MODEL T06 · TWO-TRACK ARCHITECTURE

Solving the authenticity vs. confidentiality problem.

Real 2,000-strand accounts contain sensitive client and personal history. We run two parallel tracks so Wave 1 ships immediately while Wave 2 scales without privacy risk.

TRACK A · AVAILABLE IMMEDIATELY (WAVE 1)

Redacted Real-Work "Field Notes"

Short, recorded screen captures pulled asynchronously from real team moments where Mir makes a sharp reach-back or cross-thread synthesis.

  • How it stays safe: Sensitive client names, numbers, or private details are swapped for generic equivalents in a clean HTML replay or blurred before export.
  • Zero warm-up time: Uses the 500–7,000+ strand depth the team has already accumulated.
TRACK B · SEEDED IN WAVE 1, SCALES IN WAVE 2+

Pre-Seeded "Avatar" Sandboxes

Dedicated persona accounts loaded with clean, synthetic background context and domain documents for scenario demos and live sessions.

  • Archetype 01 — The Formulator / R&D Researcher: Open literature, bench formulas, ingredient specs, multi-week experiment logs.
  • Archetype 02 — The Indie Builder / Operator: Multi-month software + ops project, architecture decisions, sprint history.
  • Archetype 03 — The Grad Student / Deep Reader: 50-book research corpus, reading notes, thesis drafts.
MINDGUT SOCIAL & CONTENT STRATEGY 05 / TWO-TRACK PRIVACY MODEL
/ 04 · THE REPEATABLE 30-SECOND CLIP SPINE T09 · STANDARDIZED FORMAT

One repeatable spine for every "Field Note from the Lab."

Contributors never have to invent a storyboard from scratch. Every short clip follows a 3-beat, 30-second spine—and can be generated directly inside Mir.

BEAT 01 · 0–5 SECONDS

The Context Card

Clean title card in the app's native palette naming the strand count and domain right up front:

STRAND 2,140 · FORMULATION R&D
"6 weeks of bench notes. Zero re-briefing."
BEAT 02 · 5–22 SECONDS

The Turn (The Proof)

Tight screen capture or animated HTML playback of a single prompt and Mir's reach-back—showing Mir connecting a decision, constraint, or file from weeks earlier without being prompted to look for it.

BEAT 03 · 22–30 SECONDS

The Contrast & End-Card

One plain-English takeaway line that names the win:

"In a stateless chat, this takes a 20-minute re-brief. Here, it took four words."
mindgut.ai · Field Notes
/ COPY-PASTE MIR PROMPT FOR ANY TEAM MEMBER

"Take the exchange we just had and package it into a self-contained HTML 'Field Note' walkthrough (16:9 and 9:16 toggle) for our social rollout. Redact any client-identifying names or proprietary numbers into clean generic equivalents while keeping the exact structural leap intact. Use a 3-part sequence: (1) Context Card showing current strand depth and domain, (2) animated playback of the prompt and the memory reach-back, and (3) a closing contrast line + mindgut.ai end-card. Use light sage ground #EEF2E7, ink #151B17, forest #126B3D, and Geist / JetBrains Mono typography."
