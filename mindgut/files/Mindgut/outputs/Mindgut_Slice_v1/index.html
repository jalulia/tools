Sources and notes

Everything on this page is a proposal: the brand, the interface and the project shown. Dana, Hollis & Vane and Marguerite are invented; every date, quote and strand is written to show the behaviour.

The behaviour shown, decisions kept with their scope and reasons across months, model switches and a change of user, is what Mindgut users report in internal sessions. It has not been independently tested, and no example here is a recorded session.

The record holds what was said and done in Mindgut. It is not everything a model knows, and it does not make any model more accurate on its own.

Model names show that the record is model-independent. They are not endorsements or benchmarks.
