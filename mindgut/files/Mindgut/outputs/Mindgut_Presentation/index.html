Sources and notes

Everything here is a proposal: brand, interface, website, social, and advertising. The tea project, Dana, Okafor Studio, Hollis & Vane, and Marguerite are invented; every date, quote, strand, and figure is written to show the behaviour.

The behaviour shown—decisions kept with their scope and reasons across months, model switches, and corrections—is what Mindgut users report in internal sessions. It has not been independently tested, and no example here is a recorded session.

The record holds what was said and done in Mindgut. It is not everything a model knows, and it does not on its own make any model more accurate.

Model names show that the record moves between models. They are not endorsements or comparisons. The website and its URL are a concept, not a live site.
