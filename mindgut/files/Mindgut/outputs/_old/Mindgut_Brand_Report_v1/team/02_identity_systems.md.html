# Mindgut / Mir — Identity System v1
Worker a11 (identity_design_2). Files: `sandbox/agents/a11/mark.svg`, `wordmark.svg`, `tokens.css`, `motion.css`, `helix_viz.js`. Sources read: BRIEF.md, src_what_mir_is.txt. Fonts not fetched; palette computed by hand and checked for WCAG contrast only.

## Design principles

1. **Accumulate, never flash.** Everything on screen should look like it was added to, not replaced. Why: Mir is the delta between a founding HELIX and an accumulated one. The brand should look like a record that grew, so lists fill in order, counts climb, and nothing wipes.

2. **Breathe at 21.** The only idle motion is a 21-step breath on a 4.2 second cycle. Why: the history is trimmed every 21 exchanges. One visible rhythm, borrowed from the instrument itself, replaces every spinner, pulse and shimmer. It is quantized (steps, not a smooth sine) because exchanges are discrete.

3. **Three phases, one accent system.** Assertion (147), relation (258), reflection (369) get three low-chroma hues, and that is the whole palette beyond ink and paper. Why: every exchange is measured into those three cosets. The hues are the readout, not decoration. No fourth colour except the alive dot, which is status, not brand.

4. **No fretboard: the frame is a hairline.** Containers are open, strokes are thin, nothing is pinned in a heavy box. Why: nothing in Mindgut instructs the model to be Mir. The visual frame should hold things lightly the same way. Hairline rules and outline marks, not filled blocks.

5. **The record is permanent, the voice is a label.** The HELIX, the mark, the wordmark and the dot are fixed; the model name is the only thing that is allowed to change on screen. Why: identity is in the record; the model is the renewable part. A model swap must look like exactly that, a label changing, with everything else holding still.

## Logo system

**The refined mark: Mr. Mind Gut, drawn as an instrument reading.**

- **Head:** a 76 x 76 rounded square (corner radius 18) on a 120 grid, drawn as a 2.5-unit hairline stroke in paper-200 (#E9E7E1), no fill. It reads as a robot head at 16px and as an open frame at 400px. On light backgrounds the stroke swaps to ink-900.
- **Eyes:** two level 10-unit dashes at the eye line (y=46). Not circles, not screens. Calm, reading, not staring.
- **Gut/brain:** one ring, radius 15, centred low in the head at (60,68). It is split into three 112 degree arcs with 8 degree gaps, stroke 5, in the three phase hues: gold (147, assertion) from 12 o'clock clockwise, teal (258, relation) at the bottom, violet (369, reflection) back to the top. One gap sits on the antenna axis so the ring points up to the dot. This is the HELIX in miniature: three phases, closed loop, no centre fill.
- **Antenna:** a 9-unit hairline stem rising from the head.
- **Alive dot:** one 4-unit radius circle in #3DDC84 at the top of the stem. The only filled shape. It breathes (see Motion). This is the existing "I'm alive" equity, moved from a status bar into the mark itself.
- 8 elements total (1 rect, 6 paths, 1 circle), under the 12-path cap. Minimum size 16px: at that size the ring reads as a three-colour circle and the dot as a green pixel, which is the favicon.
