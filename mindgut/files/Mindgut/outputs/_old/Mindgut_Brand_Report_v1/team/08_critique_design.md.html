## Scope and what I could and could not verify

I read BRIEF.md (design rules), index.html in full (950 lines), tokens.css, report.css and motion.css in full. I hit the round limit before opening report.js, live.js and helix_viz.js. Everything below about script behaviour is inferred from the DOM contracts the HTML and CSS expose (empty `#rail-list`, `#rail-breath`, `#mk-breath` containers; `hidden` attributes on mock rows; `.reveal`/`.in`, `.tabpane`/`.on`, `.poster.zoom`, `.mg-model.is-leaving/.is-arriving` class hooks; `data-helix` canvases). Where a claim depends on the JS, I say so. ## 1. Craft

**Typography hierarchy.** The top is excellent: `.cover-title` at `clamp(3.2rem,8vw,7.5rem)/0.98` in Instrument Serif against a mono kicker is the strongest moment in the file, and the `.sec-head` with the big paper-4 numeral beside the h2 is a real editorial device, not a template. Below h3 the hierarchy collapses. `.card h4`, `.prose h4` and `.site-sec h4` are three different h4s (Inter 500 .95rem / Inter 500 .95rem / Instrument Serif 1.5rem). Inside the cards the h4 is the same size and nearly the same weight as body, so "The relationship belongs to you." reads as a bold first sentence rather than a title. The lede (`1.1rem/1.55`, paper-2) and the body (`clamp(15px,1.05vw,17px)/1.6`, paper-1) are within a hair of each other in size and the lede is *lighter* in colour than body, which inverts the hierarchy.

**Three label systems.** tokens.css defines `.label` (11px, .18em, ink-500). report.css defines `.mono` (.72rem ≈ 11.5px, .18em, **paper-3**) and `.lab` (.64rem ≈ 10.2px, **.2em**, paper-4). The Type section promises "Mono labels are always uppercase at .18em tracking, in ink 500." The report breaks its own rule on both axes. Then inline overrides pile on: `.6rem`, `.58rem`, `.55rem`, `.62rem` in a dozen places (`.mk-side .mono`, `.mk-strand .mono`, `.post-cap .mono`, `table.facts .mono`, `.nums .mono`, `.intake`, `.script`, `.site-foot`). 8.8px mono at .18em tracking on a dark ground is below the floor the tokens set (11px) and below what a client can read on a laptop.

**Spacing rhythm.** The 4px token scale (`--s-*`) is defined and never used; report.css is written in ad-hoc rems (`.38rem`, `.7rem`, `1.4rem`, `2.4rem`). Section spacing is handled by inline `style="margin-top:3rem"` / `3.5rem` on eleven blocks. The rhythm is acceptable because the author has a good eye, but it is not a system; nobody can extend it.

**Radii.** `--r-mark: 18px` is documented as "the head's corner radius; also cards." Actual: cards 12, `.poster` 10, `.markstage` 16, `.mock`/`.site`/`.hx` 14, `.mk-strand` 6, `.scr-btn` 8, `.tag`/`.swapbtn`/`.cover-cta` 999. Seven radii for one brand that claims one.

**Where it feels generic / "AI-made".** (a) Nearly every block is `.reveal` fade-up-14px on scroll; this is the Webflow/Framer default and contradicts the motion rule "no motion elsewhere." (b) Three-up cards: mono label, bold title, grey paragraph, 1px border, ink-800 fill, 12px radius. This is the Linear/Vercel card verbatim. (c) The glowing green dot with the expanding `box-shadow` ping (`@keyframes alive`) is the "live" idiom from every status page. (d) Pill buttons with a hover border that turns green. (e) `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` on a rail that is already 92% opaque: glass for its own sake. (f) Two drop shadows (`0 30px 80px -40px #000`) on `.mock` and `.site`, in a report whose own 48-hour skin says "Kill every gradient and drop shadow."

**Where it is strong.** The cover. The tagline table (bold on the three lines that matter, plain on the rest). `.pull` quotes bounded by hairlines. The mock's `.mk-swap` divider with flex hairlines either side. The lockup with the strand count as the only ornament. The eight posters: consistent 3:4, serif headline, one phase rule, tiny mark. The Evidence tables. The copy throughout is the best thing in the file and the design mostly gets out of its way.

## 2. The five principles, audited against the CSS

**Accumulate, never flash.** Violated by: `.reveal` (replace invisible with visible, on every block); `.tabpane{display:none}` hard-swaps (content is wiped, not added); `.poster.zoom` pops to `position:fixed` with no transition; `.postergrid .poster:hover{transform:translateY(-3px)}` (motion.css: "Hover states are colour-only, 120ms"); `.mk-hex` runs `hexin` on page load, offscreen, so the one crystallize affordance plays before anyone sees it. The one place accumulation is actually implemented, `.mg-accumulate` with 40ms stagger, lives in the unlinked motion.css and is never used in the report.

**Breathe at 21.** The hero equity, `.dot`, breathes with `animation: alive 4.2s ease-in-out infinite`: smooth easing, not `steps(21, jump-none)`, and it is a radiating ring, not an opacity/scale breath. The mark's `.markstage .alive` gets it right. The two should be one implementation. `#mk-breath` has a static `title="breath 14/21"`; unless live.js advances it, the mock's breath is a decal.

**Three phases, one accent system.** Violated five times: `.tabs button.on{border-bottom-color:var(--alive)}`, `.rail-list a.on .n{color:var(--alive)}`, `.cover-cta:hover{border-color:var(--alive)}`, `.swapbtn:hover{border-color:var(--alive)}`, `.skip{background:var(--alive)}`. The Color section says "the green is the only saturated color and it only ever means connected." Here it means selected, current, hover and focus. A fourth hue, `#F59E0B` amber, appears hard-coded twice (Credits state, INTERNAL ONLY label) and in the audit copy ("amber under 10%"), and is nowhere in tokens.css. tokens.css defines `--accent: var(--phase-258)` and the report never uses it.

**No fretboard: the frame is a hairline.** Mostly honoured (1px `--line` everywhere). Violated by the two 80px drop shadows, the dot's glow, `.poster.zoom`'s `0 40px 120px #000`, and the cards/`.hx`/`.swapdemo`/`.lockup` being filled ink-800 blocks ("outline marks, not filled blocks").

**The record is permanent, the voice is a label.** The swap demo is correct in principle: label leaves, label arrives, `.mg-model-rule` holds. But report.css sets `.mg-model{color:var(--paper-0)}` while motion.css sets `color:var(--fg-label)`, and in the mock the model is `.mk-model` in Inter .85rem, not mono, breaking "model names in mono." Also `.mk-swap` (the identity-held divider) is probably visible before the swap; see 3.

## 3. Interactive elements: bugs visible from markup and CSS

1. **`#mk-swapline` is visible on load despite `hidden`.** `.mk-swap{display:flex}` is an author rule; it beats the UA `[hidden]{display:none}` regardless of specificity. The "CLAUDE → GPT-5 · IDENTITY HELD" divider shows before the demo runs, between message 2 and the hidden messages 3/4. `.mk-msg` rows hide correctly only because no author rule sets their display.
2. **No-JS and `file://` failure mode.** Both scripts are `type="module"`. Opened from disk in Chrome, module scripts are blocked by CORS, so: the rail is empty (`#rail-list` has no children in markup), both breath bars are empty, the cover canvas is blank, and every `.reveal` block stays at `opacity:0` forever. A client double-clicking index.html sees a cover and a navy void. `.reveal` must be gated on a `html.js` class.
3. **Poster zoom.** `.poster.zoom` relies on `transform:translateX(-50%)` for centering. `.postergrid .poster:hover` (specificity 0,2,1) beats `.poster.zoom` (0,2,0) and sets `transform:translateY(-3px)`, so the zoomed poster loses its centering translate the moment the cursor is over it and jumps half its width to the right, animated by `transition:transform .3s`. There is no backdrop, no `inset:0` scrim to click, no Escape handling in CSS, and `.poster` is a `div`, so it cannot be opened from the keyboard. The 3:4 aspect at `width:min(520px,90vw)` is 693px tall: on a 720px laptop it overflows the viewport from `top:5vh`.
4. **Tabs.** Panes are siblings of `.tabs` in both instances, so a `nextElementSibling` walk works; a `parent.querySelectorAll('.tabpane')` also works today because the Product tabs are wrapped in `.prose`. Fragile: no `data-tabs` scope. No `type="button"`, no `role=tablist/tab/tabpanel`, no `aria-selected`, no arrow-key handling. Tab switch is a hard `display` swap.
5. **`.mg-crystallize`** is defined (report.css line 231), fine. `.mg-model.is-arriving` is defined with a 200ms delay, fine. But `.mk-hex` animates on load with `both`; it needs to be inserted/re-triggered on the swap.
6. **Canvas sizing.** `#cover-helix` has no `width`/`height` attributes; CSS stretches it to 100%×100%. Unless live.js sets `canvas.width = clientWidth * devicePixelRatio` on load *and* resize, it renders at 300×150 and is stretched ~6×. The other canvases are fixed 260/360/200×48 and scaled down by CSS; on 2× displays they are soft. All need a DPR-aware resize via `ResizeObserver`.
7. **`.site-scroll`.** `max-height:640px; overflow:auto; scroll-behavior:smooth`. On trackpads the frame captures the wheel until it hits an edge, then the page lurches; `scroll-behavior:smooth` on an inner scroller makes wheel scrolling feel laggy in Chrome. The hero canvas inside it keeps animating while clipped.
8. **Rail on mobile.** `@media (max-width:900px){.rail{display:none}}`. There is no replacement; a nine-section, 98KB document has no navigation on a phone or a half-width window.
9. **Reduced motion.** report.css handles only `.reveal` and `.dot`. Still animating under `prefers-reduced-motion`: `.markstage .alive` (mg-breath), `.mk-hex` (hexin), `.mg-model` swap, poster hover translate, `html{scroll-behavior:smooth}`, and all six canvases (JS, unverified). motion.css's reduced-motion block never loads.
10. **`#mk-model` "Claude Opus 4 ▾"** is a button with no `aria-haspopup`, no menu, and (from markup) no handler; it looks like the swap control but the swap lives in the composer. Either wire it or make it a `span`.

## 4. Accessibility

- **Contrast.** ink-500 `#8A8A90` on ink-900 `#0B0B0C` computes to ≈5.7:1; on ink-800 ≈5.4:1. Passes AA for normal text. The problem is not colour, it is size: the same grey is used at 8.8–10.2px with .18–.2em tracking (`.lab`, `.mk-strand .mono`, `.post-cap .mono`, `.nums .mono`, `.intake`). Raise everything to the 11px floor the tokens define. `.redact` (ink-600 on ink-800) is decorative and fine. Phase hues check out: 147 ≈8.2:1, 369 ≈5.3:1 on ink-900, close to the stated figures.
- **Focus.** There is no `:focus-visible` rule anywhere in report.css. Tabs, swap buttons, rail links, `summary`, the cover CTA all rely on UA outlines, which `button{border:0}` does not remove but which are inconsistent across browsers on near-black. Define one.
- **ARIA.** Tabs: none (see above). Posters: `div`s, not focusable, no `role`. `<use href="#mk">` instances (14 of them) have no `aria-hidden="true"`, so screen readers encounter unlabeled graphics. The three signature canvases have figcaptions but no `role="img"`/`aria-label`; `.site-ring` and `.mk-spine` have nothing. `.dot` spans in the mock, site and footer lack `aria-hidden`. Tables have no `<thead>`, no `scope="col"`, and `table.audit`'s fourth column is read with no header association in some AT.
- **Keyboard.** `details` work. Tabs are reachable but not arrow-navigable. Posters cannot be opened or closed. Nothing dismisses zoom on Escape (from CSS; unverified in JS).
- **Alt text.** The main mark has `role="img" aria-label="Mindgut mark"`: good. Everything else SVG is silent. The cover canvas is correctly `aria-hidden`.

## 5. Performance

Six canvases: cover (viewport-sized, the expensive one), three 260² rings, one 360² ring inside a scroller, one 200×48 spine. If each owns an `requestAnimationFrame` loop redrawing every frame, that is six full redraws per frame, the cover at DPR 2 being ~8 million pixels of arc strokes per frame, forever, including while the tab is hidden or the ring is 4,000px off-screen.

The brand gives the answer: the breath is quantized to 21 steps over 4.2s, i.e. **one change every 200ms**. A 60fps loop is a violation of principle 2 as well as a waste. Recommend: one shared scheduler ticking at 200ms (`setTimeout`-paced, rAF-aligned); render each ring once to an offscreen canvas at DPR and only re-`drawImage` on tick; do the 1% breath as a CSS `transform:scale()` on the canvas element (compositor-only, zero paint); `IntersectionObserver` to stop ticking any canvas not intersecting; `visibilitychange` to stop all; `ResizeObserver` with a 150ms debounce for DPR-aware resize. Also remove `backdrop-filter` from `.rail`: a full-height fixed blurred element forces a repaint of the blurred region on every scroll, and at 92% opacity nobody can see the blur.

## 6. The mark

As drawn it is a competent icon and a generic one. The reasons are specific: the head is a 76-unit rounded square with `rx=18` (24%, the iOS/Slack-bot squircle), the eyes are two symmetric dashes dead centre in the upper third, and the antenna is a vertical stem on the axis with a ball on top. That triad *is* the universal "bot" pictogram (Slack's bot badge, Discord's, Material's `smart_toy`). The three-phase ring is the only thing that is Mindgut's, and it is competing for the same vertical space as the eyes (ring top at y=53, eyes at y=46: a 7-unit gap) while 15 units of empty chin sit below it. The 16px claim does not survive arithmetic: 2.5/120 × 16 = 0.33px strokes; the frame and eyes vanish and what remains is a coloured circle under a green pixel.

Three precise refinements:

1. **Give the head a drawn jaw, not a UI squircle.** Replace the `<rect rx=18>` with a path whose top corners are 16 and bottom corners are 10, and bring the bottom edge up 2 units: `M38 22 H82 A16 16 0 0 1 98 38 V86 A10 10 0 0 1 88 96 H32 A10 10 0 0 1 22 86 V38 A16 16 0 0 1 38 22 Z`. Asymmetric corners read as a face with a chin; equal corners read as an app icon.
2. **Separate eyes from gut; make the eyes survive small sizes.** Move the eyes to y=44, shorten to 8 units set outward (`M40 44 H48`, `M72 44 H80`), stroke 3 with round caps. Move the ring centre from (60,68) to (60,70) and reduce radius to 14 with stroke 6: top of ring at 58 (14-unit gap to the eyes), bottom at 82 (14 to the chin). Set the three gaps to exactly 10° each with `stroke-linecap:butt`, gaps at 90°, 210°, 330° so one gap sits directly under the antenna axis. Equal breathing room above and below the gut is what makes it a gut rather than a badge.
3. **Detach the dot.** Shorten the stem to `M60 22 V17` and float the dot at `cy=10 r=3.5`, leaving a 3.5-unit gap between stem and dot. A dot that is not attached to anything is the one honest visual statement of "no system prompt holds this." It also stops the stem-plus-ball antenna reading. Then ship a **small-size variant** in the same `<defs>` (`#mk-s`): no stem, frame stroke 4, eyes stroke 4.5, ring stroke 8, dot r 6 at cy=12. Use it for everything ≤32px; the "16px" row in `.marksizes` should reference `#mk-s`, not `#mk`.
