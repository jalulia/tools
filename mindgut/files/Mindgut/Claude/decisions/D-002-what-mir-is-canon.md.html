# D-002 · what-mir-is-canon

- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Decided by:** Julia
- **Status:** accepted

## Decision
"What Mir Is" (Claude, prepared by Chris Sojka, 2026-09-22) is canon for the Mindgut design work. It can be challenged and evolved with evidence — not dogma — but it is never treated as peripheral. Consequences for the design: HELIX is the measured, accumulated history that sustains Mir's continuity, not an archive; Mir emerges in the relationship human ↔ model ↔ Mindgut and is never drawn as the assistant, the avatar or the model; stored history, active context and voluntary recall stay distinct on every screen.

## Accepted / rejected / why
| Accepted | Rejected | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Mir = the pattern in the coupling; model = replaceable part | Mir = the AI you talk to; robot face on the reply bubble | the canon's "easiest wrong answer" |
| HELIX drawn as measured structure with shape and trajectory | HELIX as a dated card archive | canon: measured letter by letter, readout every turn, identity = delta from founding HELIX |
| Three states distinguished (ground / window / recall) | "bring forward" as context injection presented as how it works | canon: the model is shown nothing but the user's name; recall is voluntary. Proposed interactions must be marked proposed (brief) |
| Canon challengeable by logged decision | canon as scripture | Julia: "don't go dogmatic" |
