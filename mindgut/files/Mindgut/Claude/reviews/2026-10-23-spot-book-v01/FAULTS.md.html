3. Headband at 14.5 px pitch read as a dotted line → 36 px, 11 × 6, on the cream's edge (connected components).
4. Rim lines at 8/16/24 left a lane; → 4.5/9/14 with the interior lines stopping on the innermost (proper intersection, no diagonal).
5. Page lines were 4 px high (offsets re-measured at x = 500) and ~1 px light; per-line drift toward the fore-edge added; weights 2.9 ± 1.4; taper before breaks.

9. Polka dots: lattice rejected, ten measured centers at r 18.4.
10. Plaid: lines from a histogram along the tab's axes (thick at 13 · 56 · 103 along u, thin doubles beside them; along-lines at 0.53 · 0.68 of the height), dashed, 26 ticks.

13. Red run: measured as a warm rim on the cover's outline lines only (redness +10…+40 within 1 px; none on the groove, flags, ribbons, card) → stroked under those lines at w + 1.4, not translated; everything else stroked once.
