# Review notes · Mindgut Exhibition V01 and Collection V01

12 Nov 2026 · Claude, for Julia Compton, ø co.
Files reviewed: `Mindgut_Exhibition_V01.html`, `Mindgut_Collection_V01.html`, `Mindgut_Collection_V01.json`, `Creative_Intelligence_V01.md`.
Method: headless Chrome (Playwright, SwiftShader GL) at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844, plus reduced-motion emulation. Every chapter and all nine territories were captured and looked at; console, page errors, network requests and horizontal overflow were checked by script. Not checked: Safari, Firefox, a real phone, a real GPU, screen readers.

## Three lenses

### Creative director: does it answer the brief?
- **Holds.** The exhibition makes an argument: nine territories found in the work, two of them (The Counted Word, One Object, Two Views) proposed as the core identity, with the rest as registers. Each territory carries idea, visual DNA, formula, evidence, possibilities, critique and relationships, as the brief asks.
- **Holds.** The missing collection document has a direct answer and named candidates (archive §1). It does not claim a find it cannot show.
- **Holds.** Proposals are labeled New in the Systems chapter; the identity-versus-execution split sits in the Territories chapter, where the reader meets the territories.
- **Weak.** Chat BBT / Chat EBT, the memetic discussion the brief asks for, is absent because it was not reachable. The documents say so in every place it would appear; the gap remains.
- **Weak.** Opportunities are ranked by judgment, not by a scored rubric. Ranking criteria are stated in each entry’s text, not as a table.

### Design director: is it made well?
- **Holds.** One type system across all four files: Archivo (variable width and weight, from the W-landing assets) for everything, JetBrains Mono only for machine readouts (hashes, codec output, specs), Instrument Serif only inside the Specimen territory. No Inter, Roboto or Fraunces. No oxblood.
- **Holds.** Each territory takes its ground from its own evidence (Klein blue for The Counted Word, near-black for The Specimen, sage for Worlds), and the frame returns to white between them.
- **Holds.** The wordmark appears at signature scale only, top left of the rail and the archive header.
- **Fixed in this pass:** cover contact sheet too small; evolution matrix overflowed and wasted empty days; thumbnails vertically centered in buttons; counted-field mask text tiny; standing waves too thin in the poster and Field Note; T1 duplicated its live view with a screenshot; phone rail bled a one-word-per-line column down the left edge; tables overflowed at 390; opportunity rows squeezed to one word per line on phone; cover meta line sat under the Chapters button; count readout overlapped its field on phone; archive filter bar covered anchored entries.
- **Open.** The count lab’s field is dense at 390 wide; readable, but the mask is small. A phone-specific cell size would help.
- **Open.** The lineage diagram in the archive scrolls sideways on phone instead of reflowing.

### Creative technologist: are the demos true to their source?
- **Holds.** `record.js` is a line-for-line port of `record-system.ts` (scanZ3, makeRecord, createProjection, nodeLightness, nodeColor, recallAddresses). Check: “Pick up where you left off.” → 21 counted, channels 3 · 7 · 11, matching the Said and Kept R3 chips.
- **Holds.** The Press runs the T-04 engine unmodified: `fx.js` and `set.js` are byte-identical to `Mindgut/T-04 Effect Set` (sha256 9421446a…, 2bdf5525…), run in a worker; parameter mapping follows Codex’s material-lab adapter. Measured: ink 133–464 ms, foil 337 ms at default size.
- **Holds.** The counted field uses W-landing V01’s own field logic (band sweep, alpha, hover ring) and its simulated record, labeled as simulated.
- **Holds.** Gradient by Number and eleven other originals run as found, decompressed from the file into sandboxed frames; nothing is re-created.
- **Flagged, not changed.** Lab 1 (W-landing) and Lab 2 (record system) use different English baselines because their sources do: E0 = (0.282, 0.394, 0.324) versus μ = (0.302, 0.351, 0.347). Listed as an open decision rather than silently unified.
- **Exception, labeled.** Running an original fetches that original’s own Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com); the exhibition itself makes zero external requests. The lightbox says so beside the button.

## Known limits
- The exhibition is one 14.7 MB file so it opens offline. About 8 MB is image data parsed on load; images decode lazily, but the parse happens up front. On a slow machine the cover takes a moment.
- Screens of each piece are this session’s own renders at 1440 × 900 except where the makers’ own captures were better (Codex dossier, brand kit pages). Some originals use web fonts that the headless renderer had to fetch; where a font failed, the capture shows a fallback.
- `Her Wave.html` depends on a local app server and did not render; it is listed but has no frames. The two Design canvases need the Design runtime and are linked, not shown.
- File hashes come from the Mac (shasum through Desktop Commander) except where marked “hashed from staged copy”. One alias, `drafts/nocturne-how-moths-work.html`, has no hash.
- Mir Library 01’s date comes from its W-ferrofluid siblings; its own file date was not read.
