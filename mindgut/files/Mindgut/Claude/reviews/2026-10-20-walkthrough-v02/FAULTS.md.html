- Motion: reveal (45 ms/strand), window march (200 ms/strand), three reaches, model swaps every 1.8 s, 4.2 s pulse envelope, 90 s helix turn; reduced-motion = composed stills; M toggles.
- Usefulness: the HELIX plates show measured structure, provenance (session, coset, checksum), and the act of recall; uncertainty is not yet encoded (open for V03 — ghost strands are the only "uncertain" mark).
