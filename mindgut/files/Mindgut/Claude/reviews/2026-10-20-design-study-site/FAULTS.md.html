## Reject tests
- Logo swap: **fails at the hero** — giant tight grotesk, one red word, mono kicker, card stack on a hot panel = 2026 AI-product grammar.
- Explanation vs visual: **fails on section titles** — "Find your way back to a thought" over a card carousel; "Recognizable before it speaks" over a mark in a gray card; "Distinctive presence" is an adjective.
