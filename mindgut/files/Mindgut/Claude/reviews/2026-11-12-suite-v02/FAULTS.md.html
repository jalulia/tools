# Review notes · Suite V02 (links)

12 Nov 2026 · Claude, for Julia Compton, ø co.

## What changed from V01
- **Links are real.** Every href is relative to where the page lives in `~/Mindgut/Claude/`, so the same files work opened from the Mac and inside the published suite, which mirrors `presentation/`, `collection/` and `reviews/`. V01 used `file:///` paths, plain-text paths and links to claude.ai pages; those are gone.
- **Deck, exhibition, collection, Creative Intelligence** each carry the same suite navigation (Deck · Exhibition · Collection · Creative Intelligence · Review notes · Data).
- **Deck:** every screen is a link to the original file it shows (brand kit pages open the PDF at that page). Every family ID opens its collection entry. Every slide's footer links to the matching chapter of the exhibition or collection.
- **Exhibition:** the lightbox opens the original, the collection entry, and each source file. `#item-W21` opens a piece; `#run-W21` runs its original. The companion table links the files.
- **Collection:** each entry links its files, its exhibition entry and, where embedded, its running original.
- **claude.ai artifacts** link to the HTML copy saved in `collection/sources-2026-11-12/artifacts/` when opened from the Mac, and to the artifact itself when opened on the web.
- **On the web**, a link to a file that exists only on the Mac is dropped rather than faked; if the exhibition embeds that original, the link runs the embedded copy instead.
- **River and Riverbed** now shows Said and Kept R3 (12 Nov, 06:47), the latest version: its river and its helix. The scan-type draft (06:38) is deprecated and no longer shown. Dark SaaS screens (W-landing V02, the Island experience, the case-study screens) were taken out of the featured spots; T2, T3, the discovery slide, the tilt lab, the system wave and the poster are drawn on paper.

## Link check
- Script: `linkcheck.py` resolves every `href` in every page, plus the lightbox links the exhibition builds at runtime (from its data), against this folder and against 67 original paths confirmed present on the Mac (Desktop Commander, 12 Nov, 17:15).
- Result: deck 228, exhibition 164, collection 161, Creative Intelligence 185, index 9 links. 0 broken.
- Fragment targets checked: slide ids, exhibition chapter and territory ids, collection entry ids, `#item-`/`#run-` routes, PDF `#page=`.

## Not checked

- Whether macOS asks for permission the first time a page in `~/Mindgut` opens a file in `~/Downloads` or `~/Desktop`.
