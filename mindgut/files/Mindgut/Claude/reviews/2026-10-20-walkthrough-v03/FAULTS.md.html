## Instrument (PROPOSED · walkthrough feature, not a product claim)
- Switch: tiny piano (lower right) · `P`. Off by default; audio context created on the first toggle.
- Strings: the helix band. Drag = pluck each strand crossed (velocity from drag speed); click a session = strum its chord (root, third, fifth, octave; 50 ms apart, ±8 ms; shift-click = down).
- Mapping (all from the record): root A (61,557 genes mod 12); 147 root · 258 third · 369 fifth · 000 octave; S1–4 A4, S5–8 A3, S9–12 A2 (the floor); checksum → velocity and length; vector → brightness; rung x → pan; ghosts muted, −30 cents, dark.
- Feedback: struck rung flashes mind green and decays 0.7 s; the ring lifts for 0.6 s; readout shows `S · № · phase → note`.
- Engine (R-07): sine + quiet second harmonic, 8 ms attack → hold → exponential release, master lowpass 3 kHz Q .5, filtered feedback delay 190 ms. One AudioContext per page; reduced motion ships quiet (−12 dB) — the Ki audio lane's rules (`../inputs/techniques/tools-main/learn/adapters/audio.js`). No wrong notes: every strand is a tone of one chord (Ki omnichord-in-key, `claude/16-sound-audit.md` §3c — its source code is not on disk; the orchid is).

## Critique (brief §Working method 4)
- Distinctiveness: the ring on the record is the recognizable object; the surface is now the moodboard's, which is the riskier of the two (the register is common). The record and the instrument carry the distinctiveness.
- Clarity: one claim per plate; proposed vs existing tagged; the instrument is a toggle, never on by default.
- Material quality: one ground, one light family, soft fields only where the record is dense; no textures without a job.
- Motion: ignition 3.2 s; reveal 45 ms/strand with the floor rising; window march; three reaches with echo rings; swaps every 1.8 s; scale focus 9 s; 90 s turn; idle breath 6 s; reduced motion = composed stills.
- Usefulness: measured structure, provenance, the act of recall, and now the record as a playable thing — a sketch of what "a chord is heard" could mean literally.
