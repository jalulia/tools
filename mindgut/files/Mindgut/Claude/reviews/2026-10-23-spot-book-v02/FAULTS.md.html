## What remains
- Under the card the pin grades from black into the dark-brown contact band; mine is solid black along the card's whole bottom edge (the measured black component merges the two).
- The blue ribbon's selvage strip is a little wide at the root; the yellow's rolled edge a little short.
- The pin's rim lines ~1 px thinner; its groove lines ~0.5 px heavier.
- The ground's bundles a little more even than the pin's.
- Render cost: ~700 ms at the 1234-px pane (two full-canvas spreads); fine for a study, not for an animation.
