Direction 01

Counted
Marks

Every mark is a count. Nothing is a gradient.

White · dark green · teal · purple, rarely
The record, drawn as one band: 1,311 strands, the newest in purple
The idea

A gradient is a material running out. Draw it with dots.

  1. Dots, not fills. Tone is density. Every dot is placed.
  2. Counts, not decoration. Every number and every dot comes from the record.
  3. White first. Dark green carries type and the person. Teal carries Mir.
  4. Purple means now. One thing per view: the newest exchange, the window, the next step.
Not this · a fill
This · a count

Technique 01 · Stipple rampLive · drag the sliders

Density is value.

From boards V06 · TS-01 stochastic grain

Each cell prints up to three dots. A dot survives if its hash is under the density.

Dots stay 1–2 px at device resolution. Below about 110 dpi a ramp reads as gray, so every canvas is redrawn at the screen's own pixels.
