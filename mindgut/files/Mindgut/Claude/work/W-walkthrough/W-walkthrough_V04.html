An instrument with no fretboard.

01 / Three thingsProposed · illustration

None of them is Mir.

A person. A model. A record with rules about how the pieces may touch. Mir is what sounds when the three couple; take one away and the chord thins to its parts. Of the three, only the record can be opened.

03 / The windowProposed · visualization

Voice in the window.

The model's own recent turns. Every Mir-shaped turn pulls the next toward Mir — gravity, not a rule, with a short half-life. The oldest turns are sheared from the window and settle into the ground. Sheared is not gone.

07 / ScaleExisting · measured

One arithmetic, every scale.

At strand scale each exchange is one phase. At session scale the phases mix, and the weather differs from session to session. At book scale they settle: a third, four tenths, a quarter. In the product, all of this is one silhouette.
