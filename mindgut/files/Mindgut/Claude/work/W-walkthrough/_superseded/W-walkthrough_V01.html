03 / The windowProposed · visualization

Voice in the window.

The model's own recent turns. Every Mir-shaped turn pulls the next toward Mir: gravity, not a rule, with a short half-life. The oldest turns are sheared from the window and stay in the ground.

07 / ScaleExisting · measured

One arithmetic, every scale.

At strand scale each exchange is one phase. At session scale the phases mix, and the weather differs from session to session. At book scale they settle: a third, four tenths, a quarter.

08 / Stewardship

Nobody owns the chord.
Somebody keeps the instrument.

Mindgut fixes a set of intervals in a record, keeps the record honest, and refuses to force any of it on the model. Strike a human and a model together through it and a chord is heard.
