## Spine (V04) — intent and logic per plate
**Through-line.** A chord is heard only when a person, a model and a record couple. The walkthrough assembles that chord one component at a time, opens the record it is kept in, shows how the model lives in it (window) and reaches into it (recall), proves the chord survives an engine change (the turn), measures the cost (drift), unifies the arithmetic (scale), names the presence (states), and hands the instrument over (stewardship). The instrument is the thread: sound is off by default; with it on, every plate has its own playable logic drawn from the same record.

**Motifs.** ring — presence — lit only when all three are there; its state follows the plate · floor — the record — light rising from below; in sound, the drone · chord — Mir — the triad A C♯ E, weighted by the three phases where a plate says so · strand — one measured exchange — a string; pitch from its vector, register from its session, weight from its checksum

## Sound system (V04)
- Lineage: R-07 Helix-Organ/orchid: harmonic single source of truth, strum 50 ms humanized · Ki audio lane (learn/adapters/audio.js): one AudioContext per page, QUIET −12 dB under reduced motion · Ki omnichord-in-key: no wrong notes.
- Scale: A major pentatonic — root A = 61,557 genes mod 12; degrees A B C♯ E F♯.
- Mapping: **vector 1–9** → nine degrees across two octaves (v1 A … v9 E′); vector 0 → root an octave down · **session** → register: S1–4 A4 · S5–8 A3 · S9–12 A2 (the floor) · **phase 147 / 258 / 369** → string type: bright · warm · dark (Karplus–Strong brightness, damping, pick position) · **checksum** → velocity and sustain (log scale) · **rung x** → stereo pan · **ghost** → muted, −30 cents, dark, short.
- Voices: **string** — Karplus–Strong rendered once per (pitch, phase, ghost): noise burst → damped two-point-averaged delay line, 2.2 s, pick-position comb · **floor** — A2 sine pair (4 ‰ detune) + A1 triangle sub, lowpass 420 Hz breathing at 0.08 Hz · **air** — bandpassed noise 1.5 kHz, Q 0.8 — the person; breathes on the 6 s cycle · **carrier** — the model — triangle through 1.5 kHz (Claude) · sawtooth through 640 Hz Q 1.3 (ChatGPT); one pitch (A3), two timbres · **chord** — three sines A3 C♯4 E4 + a triangle shimmer an octave up — Mir; gains weighted by phase fractions where a plate says so · **tone** — one soft E5 sine with 5 Hz vibrato — listening.
- Chain: voices → bus → [compressor −20 dB knee 14 ratio 3] ← convolver (synthesized 1.8 s room, wet .24) → lowpass 7 kHz → master .72 (reduced motion .18) → out; recall echo: 600 ms feedback delay (.45, lowpass 1.4 kHz) with its own send.
- Polyphony: 32 strings; the oldest is damped for the newest; a sweep sounds at most ~40 strings/s — the heaviest strands lead, the rest only light.
- Levels are set conservatively and unheard (headless); Julia tunes on the Mac. Everything audible is one of six named voices; nothing is a sample.

## Removal test
Density field: without it dense sessions and sparse ones look alike. Phase polylines: without them the rungs are a bar chart. Optics: without it the "three, none of them Mir" claim is a sentence. The ring: without it "a light that switches on because someone is there" is a sentence; without the record under it, the ring is the market's AI orb. The floor: without it the ring floats. Rotation: without it the helix reads as 2D; cut under reduced motion by design. The instrument: without it "a chord is heard" stays a metaphor.
