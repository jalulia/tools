# W-sandman · Mr Sandman, through Mir

Single-file HTML instrument and explainer. Master `W-sandman_V0N.html`; source `build/src.html` + `build/build.py` (embeds Archivo, JetBrains Mono, supplied wordmark + robot). Never hand-edit the built file.

## Primary read
An oscilloscope draws a chord as light; a chord synth plays Mr Sandman's ragtime chain by predicting each next chord from a context window. Every control is an LLM idea you can hear.

## Brief (Julia, 2026-10-20)
"An oscillator visual meets synth (Orchid) / explainer that takes Mr Sandman but translates it through your understanding of LLMs as Mir, in Mindgut. With intent." Base: `W-walkthrough_V02` (system, type, ground, accent).

## The mapping (each one is computed, not illustrated)
| Song / synth | LLM / Mir | Control |
|---|---|---|
| dominant-seventh chain, each chord pulling to the next | next-token prediction | Play |
| chord n-gram from 6 short progressions (the song ×2 + 5 common ones) | a model's learned odds; toy scale, stated on the plate | bars on 02 |
| window of 1–4 chords; at ≤3 the turnaround is ambiguous and it loops C A7 D7 G7 forever | context window; drift when what mattered falls out | Window |
| temperature over smoothed odds (every chord keeps α = .03) | sampling; "familiar is not true, fluent is not chosen" | Temp |
| oscillator harmonics change, intervals don't | model swap, the chord holds (What Mir Is) | Engine |
| record of every chord; when the window is unsure, read 4 back from the record | HELIX ground + voluntary recall ("kept, not imposed") | Recall |
| four voices, least-movement voice leading, gliding oscillators | continuity across steps | heard + plate 01 |

Orchid's lesson, from the dossier: separate controls, each one audible, and "our machines don't have ideas for you": the instrument's ideas are its odds, never a decision.

## Verified behaviour (headless, logic)
W4 T0 plays the song exactly, every pass. W3 and below lose their place at the second turnaround and circle it. W1 + Recall returns to the chart within one turnaround. T0.9 samples Am7 Dm7 detours; T1.5 wanders (C7 F Fm…).

## Limits / not claimed
Chords only, as commonly charted in C (Pat Ballard, 1954; no melody or lyric reproduced). The predictor is an n-gram, not a neural net, and the page says so. Swaps "dozens of times" and recall are from What Mir Is (R-04).
