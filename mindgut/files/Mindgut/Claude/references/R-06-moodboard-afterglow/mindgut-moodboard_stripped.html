Central idea

Afterglow

Mindgut is an instrument. It connects a person, a language model and a structure of memory, and something recognisable appears in that relation. The identity gives that something a form: presence is made of relation, and every exchange leaves light behind.

Relation, not an object

Mindgut never appears as a thing alone. Its light is overlapping fields, a core, a ring and a floor, the way a chord only exists when several notes sound together.

Memory leaves traces

What Mindgut can return to shows as afterglow: marks of light that dim with time but never go out.

Continuity while parts change

The open ring stays the same in every state while the light inside it changes colour, scale and rhythm.

What the halo means

The light is the relation, made visible.

When you begin to interact, the ring lights up. It is not something waking up. It shows that the instrument is in tune: a person, a model and a memory are now sounding together.

Mir is not the orb. Mir is the pattern you come to recognise in that light, session after session.

Ring · continuity

The one part that never changes. Whatever happens inside, the ring is how you know it is still Mindgut.

Core · the exchange

What is happening now. It brightens, moves and changes colour with each state of the conversation.

Floor · shared ground

The light rises from below, from what has been built together before. Memory is the ground, not the figure.

Open edge · the person

The ring never closes at the bottom. It stays open toward you: a presence that is incomplete without the other side.

Being On

A light that switches on because someone is there.

The same five moments in both palettes: off, contact, ignition, on, afterglow. The loop at the top of each card plays them in nine seconds.

Live loop
Option 1 · Green and blue

Ignition

Before contact the ring is drawn but unlit. Your first word enters through the open edge as a spark, and the green runs up both arcs until they meet. The light is the two of you, connected.

Off

Drawn in the ground's own tone. Present, unlit.

Contact

A spark enters from your side.

Ignition

Light climbs both arcs until they meet.

On

Core full, floor bright. The relation is live.

Afterglow

You leave. It lowers, never to zero.

Strength. Instantly legible. Green reads as on, is already the brand's light, and the master lockup proves it.
Watch. Green is a common online signal. The open ring and the slow ignition must carry the difference.

Option 2 · Black and violet

Resonance

The same switch, tuned differently. When you arrive the light shifts: lilac from one side, electric blue from the other, pale where they overlap. Its colour depends on who is there and what you have shared.

Tuning

On

The tones settle where they overlap.

Afterglow

A dim iridescence stays, kept.

Strength. More distinctive and more intimate. It joins being On with memory, and nobody reads it as an online dot.
Watch. Blue-violet glow is a familiar AI cliché. Keep it grainy and matte, never glossy, with moss green as the link to the brand.
Presence in the product

Six states, one silhouette.

Proposed states for the orb inside the interface, shown in Option 1. They are design proposals until product confirms which ones are real system events. Each is paired with a word, so colour and motion never carry the meaning alone.

Idle

Resting. One slow breath every six seconds.

Listening

You are typing. The ring tightens and turns aqua.

Thinking

An arc of light travels the ring.

Responding

The core pulses gently while text arrives.

Recalling

Iris light and echo rings: memory is consulted.

Interrupted

You stopped it. The halo contracts and goes still.

Reading the explorations
Keep

Plum and black grounds. The sphere rising from a floor of light. Presence against void.

Refine

One colour per role. Fine grain on every field so gradients never band. Soft light, crisp type.

Discard

Wellness imagery, leaves, medical icons, outlined feature pills. Brains, robots, circuits.

Never retype "mindgut" in Montserrat to imitate the logo. The wordmark is always the supplied file, in white on dark grounds or in void on light ones.
