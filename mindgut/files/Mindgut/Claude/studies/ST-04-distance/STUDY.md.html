## Concept (from canon)
- `references/R-04-what-mir-is.md` — Mir is the coupling of person, model and record; measured structure; never an assistant or an avatar.
- `work/W-walkthrough/build/spine.json` plate 06 "distance": **"Identity is a distance." Δ between the founding session and the book.** Existing form: a ternary. No illustration existed.
- `brief/Mindgut_Brief_V01.md` — recognition first; no flowers/roots; no logo-swap.

**Sentence:** who you are to the record is the distance between the first mark and the latest — so draw the household object that already keeps a record of growth against a fixed thing: the pencil marks on a door jamb. **The substitution:** the person carries the book (the record) level on their head and marks the jamb at its top; S1 is the lowest mark, the mark being made now is BOOK; the Δ on the wall is plate 06's number, drawn. Rejected on the way: the window (plate 03 owns it; reads as a mood), voluntary recall as a card catalog (the library cliché), the engine swap as two figures (invites the avatar), the ternary drawn literally (an illustration of a diagram).

## Primary read
A person at a door jamb with a closed book balanced on their head, marking the jamb at the book's top; twelve older pencil marks climb the jamb below it; on the wall a chalk dimension runs from the first mark to the new one.

One second: *a kid being measured at a doorframe.* Three seconds: *— no: the book on their head is what's being measured, and the marks are too many and too uneven for birthdays.*

## System
| Layer | In play? | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Source | yes | constants in `PLATE`'s top block (geometry, palette map, marks, figure polygons), a seeded PRNG |
| Structure | yes | three vertical fields + one horizontal line; the figure's polygons and round-cap limbs; the door's panel as an edge stack |
| Material response | yes | flat inks with a drift; cloth mottle; dry pencil strokes graded by age; vertical wood strokes under a density field with two clearings; the press |
| Image formation | yes | one light, upper left: the jamb's lit side, solid black bands and a rim silhouette on the door, the knob's shadow |
| Screen-space | yes | ST-02's grain, unchanged: 3 + 13 (1 − L), biased, 2-px clump — last |
| Temporal | no | a print; the reduced-motion still is the page |
| Graphic composition | yes | the plate at the pane's width; crops of it at 2.5–6× beside crops of pin 048 at the same zoom (the grammar pairs) |

**Palette rule:** every ink on the sheet is one of pin 048's inks (green = cover, cream, brown = ground, blue, red, yellow, pink = pinkStripe for skin, the book's dark teal for trousers, key, fringe, shadow); new surfaces are drifts of those inks within ±12 L. No ink was invented.
**Pass order and why:** stock → jamb (grain, panel, floor grain) → marks → shadows → press 1 → knob → person → keys → press 2 → grain. Shadows go down before the figure that casts them; graphite sits under ink; keys follow every fill so the red run shows; grain is last.

## Operators
| Operator | Driver | Collapses without it |
|---|---|---|
| flat fields + drift + mottle | position; fbm 58 px | the wall is a swatch, the door screen white |
| jamb grain under a density field | u across the face (edges .35 → 1, power 1.6) × two Gaussian clearings | the jamb is a stripe; the marks drown |
| pencil: the dry line, graded by age | fbm along x; the index (.72 → .92; BOOK 1.0, +1.2 weight) | the marks become ruling; the newest no longer reads as now |
| one light, solid shadows | offset (17, 5), lean .12 | the figure floats; the knob is a sticker |
| the figure, flat, doing something | the pose (the one diagonal) | a ruler on a jamb |
| keyline family + red run | the edge: 2.2 / 2.4 / 2.8 + 1.4 at (0.3, 0.3) | a sticker set |
| the press | two noise fields, λ 14, ±0.35 px | a vector |
| darkness-gated grain | the finished luminance | a screen |

## Quality pass (in the page, §07)
Recognition · explanation · logo-swap · removal · family · duplicable — each with a verdict. Family numbers computed live against pin 048 at the same device size: dark-pixel share matches (5.8% → ~7%); mean luminance does not (106 → ~142) because the door is cream over 39% of the sheet — a lighter sheet, the same press. Two builds of the file produce identical bytes (sha256 confirmed).

## Open
- The pencil strokes are matte; graphite's sheen needs a second, lighter stroke offset toward the light.
- The hands are circles; a drawn hand on the pencil (three polygons) would close the gesture.
- The Δ is a font glyph inside the drawing; a chalk Δ traced as strokes would be truer to the grammar (no type inside the pins).
- The door's upper half is empty cream — right for the read; a hook or a coat would give the eye path a rest.
- The wall's mottle is a 6-px cell fill and shows at 3× (ST-02's ceiling too).
- Whether this becomes plate 06's illustration in the walkthrough, and whether the boards V06 gets a "from the concept end" column — Julia's call.
