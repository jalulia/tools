## What the boards' B-10 rebuild got wrong (V05, `rebuilds.js` R.spot)
- A top-down flat polygon book; the pin is an oblique view of the tail edge with a rounded back.
- Hatch on the cover and ground as shading; the pin has no shading hatch — its lines are the pages and the wood.
- Ribbons as parallelograms falling right; they are swept splines falling down and forward, with selvages, a rolled edge and cut ends.
- No cast shadows, no light; the pin's shadows are solid black from one upper-right light.
- Keyline as a single black stroke; the pin's cover outline is warm black over a red run.

## System
| Layer | In play? | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Source | yes | measured constants (geometry, palette, line offsets, shadow polygons), a seeded PRNG |
| Structure | yes | oblique axes u (−24.5°) · v (46.9°) · vertical; the back as a Catmull-Rom through 19 cream-envelope points; outward normals for the edge face, inward offsets for the rim lines and headband |
| Material response | yes | flat inks with a tonal drift; cloth mottle (fbm); dry-brush lines (weight/alpha from noise, taper, breaks); wood strokes under a density field |
| Image formation | yes | one light, upper right: solid black cast shadows (measured polygons), a contact band |
| Screen-space | yes | per-reference-pixel grain, amplitude 3 + 13 (1 − L), biased +10% — last |
| Temporal | no | a print does not animate; reduced motion is the still |
| Graphic composition | yes | the plate at the pane's width; the studies as crops at 1.6–5× beside the same crop of the pin |

**Shared causes:** the axes (pages, floor boards, flags and the hinge all share u or v); the light (every shadow, the contact band, the ribbons' lit/shadow edge weights); the grain's amplitude is read from the finished luminance.
**Pass order and why (V02):** ground → contact → cover (dark face, green, lit strips) → block fills (cream, cyan) → press 1 (shadows, the block's lines, the book's keylines — drawn on their own canvas, spread ±0.35 px, laid on) → objects (measured contours) → press 2 (the objects' keylines, spread) → grain. Shadows precede the objects that cast them because they lie on the stock and the block; keylines follow every fill so the red run shows beside the fill; grain is last because it is a reproduction artifact over everything and reads the finished luminance.
**Lane:** Canvas2D, one renderer in reference pixels with a view transform; every study is the same code cropped. Canvases sized from the pane's width at DPR ≤ 2.

## Measured (how)
Colour-class row scans for the ribbons, flags and card; the cream envelope (closed mask) for the back; connected components for the polka dots, headband capsules and shadows; a histogram along the plaid tab's two axes for its lines; a structure tensor for the wood-stroke angle (−21°, along u — not −6.5° as first read); column cross-sections for the edge stacks (tail edge: thin line 2.5 · green 4 · dark 9 · line 2 · cyan 3.5 · cream = 21 px; bottom: line 2 · dark teal 15 · line 2 · lit strip 9 · keyline 5 = 33 px; back: line 2 · dark 12 · line 2 at the apex); dark-pixel counts per region to calibrate the ground. Rejected on the way: a half-ellipse back (the shoulder is flat at the top, round at the bottom), a lattice for the dots (they are hand-placed), a translated copy for the red ribbon's shadow (the displacement shrinks with height), a 14.5 px headband (that is a dotted line), a −6.5° floor (it runs with the book), a hook at the groove end (the two groove lines are the cover's own outline lines and simply turn).

## Composition and time
Focal event: the ribbons leaving the pages. Negative space: the cover's green and the floor's clearing. Type does the ledger's work only (mono = the machine's values; Archivo = the notes). No motion; the reduced-motion still is the page.
