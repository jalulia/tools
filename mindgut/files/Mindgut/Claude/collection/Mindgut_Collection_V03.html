How to read an entry

Discussed, made, developed: the brief’s distinction, applied to every family.

discussedDiscussed or specified: a conversation, brief or strategy that set direction without being a design of its own (or, for the canvases, linked but not rendered). 5
madeMade once: a finished piece with no recovered predecessor. 14
developedDeveloped: iterated through recovered versions (V01 → V08, draft → final, R1 → R3). 17
recordRecord or reference: an index, staging review, report, case study or supplied asset that documents work rather than proposes a direction. 7

Status is the maker’s or the folder’s own word (canonical, directional, historical, proposal…). Paths open on the Mac they came from. Origin entries (October) are context for the week, not part of it.
