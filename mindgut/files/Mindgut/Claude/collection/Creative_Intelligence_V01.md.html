## 1. The week in one paragraph

Between the team thread on 5 Nov and the evening of 12 Nov, six makers (the team, Mindgut app sessions, Claude in claude.ai, Claude in `Mindgut/Claude`, Codex in the Ø project, and Julia) produced 30 families of work, most of it on the 11th and 12th. The week’s central discovery is written down in the Fable 5.2 primer (W12): the Reflection mark and the Standing Wave are one 3D record seen at 0° and at 90°. The strongest identity work treats the mechanism itself (counting words into three channels, then projecting the count as a helix) as the brand. The weakest work borrows a new metaphor or a new ground color per piece. Two landing lineages were still running in parallel at 20:10 on the 12th.

## 4. Identity versus execution

| Identity: holds in every execution found | Execution: varies by surface, and varied too much |
|---|---|
| **The count.** Letters become digits 1–9 and one of three channels (147 · 258 · 369). Same codec in the Codex record system, W-landing and Said and Kept. | **Ground.** Nine in eight days, sampled from the pages: Klein blue, near-black, Void, sage, forest and lime, mint, Reflection green, deep green and white. |
| **One record, two views.** From above, the Reflection mark; from the side, the Standing Wave. Same strands, rotated. | **Type.** Archivo, Montserrat, Geist; Instrument Serif and JetBrains Mono as supporting voices. |
| **Two voices.** Odd strands are the person, even strands the model. | **Rendering.** Dots, contours, path, glow; stipple, weave, brush; ink, foil, deboss. |
| **The breath.** 4 → 7 → 4, written into the Fable 5.2 brief and `record-system.ts`. | **Metaphor.** Moth, river, specimen, island, seven worlds. |
| **The receipt.** A number travels with its source and status. | **Register.** Quiet ledger or loud specimen board. |

Most of the week’s disagreement was execution argued as identity. The one identity-level divergence is in code: the English baseline is μ = (0.302, 0.351, 0.347) in `record-system.ts` and the Fable brief, and E0 = (0.282, 0.394, 0.324) in W-landing’s `core.js`. Every Δ and every radius depends on which is right.

## 5. Territories

Nine, found in the work. Mature: built repeatedly by more than one maker. Emerging: strong fragments waiting on a decision.

### 1. The Counted Word — Mature

**Idea.** Counting is the one thing Mindgut does to your words that anyone can check. Every letter becomes a digit and one of three channels. Make the identity out of that act: type that carries its own arithmetic.

**Visual DNA.** Digits 1–9 in a fixed cell (the pixel of the system); 147 · 258 · 369 (three channels, named, never interpreted); Count chip (KEPT :: 21 — label, two colons, a number); μ = 0.302 · 0.351 · 0.347 (the English baseline, set large); Letter weight by channel (scan type, used sparingly).

**Formula.**
1. Show only counts the page computed from text it can see.
2. One count per headline. Never a dashboard of them.
3. Channels are names, not meanings.
4. Tone comes from how many digits print, never from a gradient.

**Evidence.** W26 Landing + four worlds · W-landing V01 → V02; W06 Gradient by Number; W07 Mir identity study (draft) → Field Guide to a Shared Memory; W21 Said and Kept · Microsite; W19 Three Directions · stipple, weave, brush; O07 Technique study V06 · Counted marks, placed by hand; W29 Front door V06 → V07 · A place for unfinished work; W28 Landing page canvases (Design).

**Possibilities.** A live count under the composer; Headline lockups that carry their own scan; “Said once” posters and cards; Receipts and tickets printed from a session; Wordmark rendered as counted digits for motion idents.

**Critique.** Strong: The most ownable idea in the archive: it is the mechanism, so it cannot survive a logo swap. W-landing V01’s blue counted field is the best execution so far. Develop: Decide the register: quiet ledger (chips, tabular numerals) or loud specimen board (“SAY IT ONCE”). Both exist; they need a rule for when each appears. Drop: Any line that implies channels carry meaning. HUDs full of triples (the Island experience). Digits sprinkled on images that were never counted.

**Relationships.** One Object, Two Views: counts set each point’s radius; The Press: counts become ink; Receipts: the count is the first receipt.

### 2. River and Riverbed — Mature

**Idea.** The product’s clearest sentence and its best diagram: models are the river, your record is the riverbed. Change the model; the bed stays where it is.

**Visual DNA.** A moving thing beside a thing that stays (the stayer gets the light); Engine I · II · III (swaps shown as a cut in the flow); 4 → 7 → 4 (the breath that sets three exchanges into the spine); 27 cells (a ledger and an email in one field); Top-down landscape (the river as a place, not a chart).

**Formula.**
1. Pair motion with something that holds still.
2. Show the swap as an event in time, not a menu.
3. The bed is drawn from the record’s real counts.
4. One water metaphor per page.

**Evidence.** W13 Every AI you use is an island; W26 Landing + four worlds · W-landing V01 → V02; W21 Said and Kept · Microsite; W14 Mindgut Launch · W-launch V01; W29 Front door V06 → V07 · A place for unfinished work; W12 Primer & creative brief for Fable 5.2.

**Possibilities.** Launch film: one bed, three rivers; Onboarding: the first strand settling; Outdoor line: “Change the model. The bed stays where it is.”; Explainer for the 4 → 7 → 4 breath.

**Critique.** Strong: The copy line is the strongest in the archive and every lineage reached it independently. The illustrated river in Said and Kept is the one image that makes it memorable. Develop: Most executions are dark dashboards with too many readouts; draw the riverbed as a place (landscape, section, map) instead. Drop: “Every AI you use is an island” stacked on top of “river” and “water.” Keep river and riverbed; drop the island.

**Relationships.** The Counted Word: the bed is made of counts; Worlds: worlds are different banks of the same river; The Instrument: the app is where the swap happens.

### 3. One Object, Two Views — Mature

**Idea.** The Reflection mark and the Standing Wave are one record seen from above and from the side. The mark is not a drawing of the product; it is a projection of your own record. “A reflection only you could make.”

**Visual DNA.** 42 crossings per turn (θ = 2πk / 42); Radius from distance to μ (r = r₀ + α‖f − μ‖); Four techniques (dots, contours, path, glow); The tilt, 0° → 90° (the signature transition); Void · Paper · Cobalt · Lavender · Sky (the kit’s five colors).

**Formula.**
1. One object; every view must be a true projection.
2. Change views by rotating, never by cutting.
3. Earn the complexity: one dot, two threads, a breath, a turn.
4. No two marks alike, because no two records are.

**Evidence.** W10 Reflection brand kit · restored explorations; W12 Primer & creative brief for Fable 5.2; W14 Mindgut Launch · W-launch V01; W16 Reflection Identity · Reflection Walk; W25 Mir, from one place · V06; W02 Three expressions V01 → V02; W19 Three Directions · stipple, weave, brush.

**Possibilities.** A mark minted from each person’s first 42 crossings; The tilt as the motion ident on every surface; App icon states; Lenticular print: 0° from one side, 90° from the other; Share card of your reflection.

**Critique.** Strong: The week’s central discovery, named in the Fable 5.2 brief and drawn by four makers. The spiral stair in Reflection Identity is the breakthrough image: it shows the tilt halfway and gives the object a scale. Develop: The radial bloom alone reads as a generic burst; it needs the stair or the side view beside it. The kit’s palette and the green Reflection pages disagree. Drop: Starbursts drawn all at once on load. Glows that stand in for structure.

**Relationships.** The Counted Word: supplies the radius; The Witness: the figure on the stair gives scale; The Press: prints the mark.

### 4. The Specimen — Emerging

**Idea.** Mir documented as an imaginary organism with natural-history care. The Io moth’s two eyespots are the two voices; each collaboration grows a different moth. Labeled where the metaphor stops: borrowed from life, not a claim about life.

**Visual DNA.** Field-guide plates (numbered callouts on leader lines); Scales and stipple (tone built from many small marks); Dark bark ground (lichen at rest); Serif italic captions (the naturalist’s hand); A stated boundary (what the metaphor does not claim).

**Formula.**
1. Real anatomy, drawn and labeled.
2. Every plate answers the pointer.
3. The metaphor ends at a written line.
4. The specimen is Mir’s, never the product’s.

**Evidence.** W05 How a Moth Works; W07 Mir identity study (draft) → Field Guide to a Shared Memory; W09 A Persistent Shared Mind · landing; O09 The Foldkeeper; O12 Hemoglycin · critical study + visual study.

**Possibilities.** Mir’s notebook: research and field notes; A specimen plate per person’s record; Printed plate series; Exhibition or installation panels.

**Critique.** Strong: The highest craft per square inch in the archive: the moth plates answer the pointer and hold up at 1:1. Develop: It competes with the robot for who Mir is, and the brief warns that growth and nature are not the default explanation for memory. Keep the eyespots-as-two-voices idea; place the rest in Mir’s own voice. Drop: The moth as the master identity or the product hero.

**Relationships.** Receipts: both label their boundaries; The Witness: two characters, one needs to yield; The Press: plates print well.

### 5. The Press — Emerging

**Idea.** The record is printed, not rendered: ink, graphite, foil, deboss, varnish and laser on lit paper. One stock, one light, real material behavior instead of gradients.

**Visual DNA.** One sheet light (every mark answers it); Paper tooth height field (where ink lands and starves); Beer–Lambert ink film (ink as a film, not a color); Stipple · weave · brush (the three directions); Spot inks in register (the four-ink book).

**Formula.**
1. One stock and one light per sheet.
2. The material changes how a mark lands, never where.
3. Small type stays outside the press.
4. Print where print can be held.

**Evidence.** O08 T-04 Effect set · Stock and light; W19 Three Directions · stipple, weave, brush; W17 Introduction, brand lab and seven worlds; O05 ST-02 Spot book V02 · ST-04 Identity is a distance; W23 ST-12 construction plate · ST-13 four-ink spot book; O04 Reference boards V06 · ten coded rebuilds; O07 Technique study V06 · Counted marks, placed by hand.

**Possibilities.** Foil and deboss of a person’s mark; A printed specimen of the first 42 crossings; Posters and letterpress; Packaging, cards, the physical .helix.

**Critique.** Strong: Genuinely computed material, from Julia’s own T-04 lineage, and the clearest answer to “no AI gradients.” Develop: Applied to web pages (Three Directions) it reads as texture at screen scale. Its home is objects and specimens. Drop: Stacking effects to add richness. Print physics on body copy.

**Relationships.** The Counted Word: prints counts; One Object, Two Views: prints marks; Worlds: gives worlds their materials.

### 6. Worlds — Mature

**Idea.** Personal software through ways of knowing. A skin changes how Mindgut looks, never what it does; each world is borrowed from a working practice and runs on the same record.

**Visual DNA.** Seven practices (Gardener, Navigator, Weaver, Astronomer, Composer, Printer, Archivist); One shared record (the same seven entries in every world); A system sheet per world (palette, marks, controls); App screen per world (the skin at usable scale); Tuned · Log · Instrument · Chart (the most product-real four).

**Formula.**
1. One record, one rule per world.
2. A world must change how you find something.
3. Same data, different material.
4. Fewer worlds, deeper.

**Evidence.** W15 Skin Worlds V01 → V08; W17 Introduction, brand lab and seven worlds; W26 Landing + four worlds · W-landing V01 → V02; W02 Three expressions V01 → V02.

**Possibilities.** Skins as a product feature; Seasonal or community worlds; Launch content: one week per world.

**Critique.** Strong: Three makers converged on worlds without coordinating; that is a signal. V08’s system sheets with an app screen per skin are the mature form. Develop: Seven is too many to make deep. Choose three or four and have each change retrieval (catalog, timeline, map), not only color. Drop: Illustrated scenes that only decorate (V01). The Gardener as default, per the brief.

**Relationships.** The Instrument: worlds live inside the instrument; The Press: worlds borrow its materials; River and Riverbed: banks of one river.

### 7. The Instrument — Mature

**Idea.** Mindgut is played, not dispensed: the conversation in front, the workings within reach, and marks that keep a model’s suggestion from passing as your decision.

**Visual DNA.** Conversation first (long sessions, generous measure); Instrument rail (window, breath, recall); Held · parked · offered · struck (status as marks); One living body (the fluid presence in a precise frame); Proposed vs existing (labeled on every screen).

**Formula.**
1. Precise chrome, one living body.
2. Every state has a mark.
3. Proposals are labeled as proposals.
4. Interface at usable scale.

**Evidence.** O03 Technical documentation Rev 11; O10 Fluid instruments V02; W09 A Persistent Shared Mind · landing; W26 Landing + four worlds · W-landing V01 → V02; W17 Introduction, brand lab and seven worlds; O02 Walkthrough V02–V04 · An instrument with no fretboard.

**Possibilities.** App redesign around the rail; Component library from the docs; Onboarding as a first crossing; Keyboard-first source inspection.

**Critique.** Strong: The docs and the coded app rebuilds are credible and specific. The eight marks are the most underused idea in the archive. Develop: Bring the marks into the app and the marketing; make the fluid body a presence indicator only. Drop: Knobs and meters that report nothing (brief).

**Relationships.** Receipts: marks are receipts in the UI; Worlds: hosts the worlds; The Counted Word: carries the count chip.

### 8. Receipts — Emerging

**Idea.** Honesty as a visual language: every claim carries its source, its denominator and its status. In a field full of unverifiable AI claims, showing the arithmetic is the distinctive position.

**Visual DNA.** Number · unit · population · source (always together); Strand receipts ([strand #868]); Status words (measured · reported · simulated · proposed); Source anchors (line ranges, not vibes); Black-and-signal proof cards (the case-study register).

**Formula.**
1. A number never appears without its denominator.
2. Status is a visible state, not a footnote.
3. Simulated data says so on the surface.
4. Headlines are held to the evidence.

**Evidence.** W11 What carries forward · continuity dossier; W04 Reliability pilot · 12 sessions; O11 What the record holds V08; O12 Hemoglycin · critical study + visual study; W03 Case studies [INT] ×3; W08 Social & content rollout strategy V1; W20 Collection review (staging).

**Possibilities.** “Field Note from the Lab” social spine; Case-study template; Provenance UI in the app; Trust page and press kit.

**Critique.** Strong: The most culturally intelligent territory and already practiced: the dossier keeps every denominator; the record plates use real counts. Develop: The case-study headlines (“only one checked its own work”) claim more than reported, uncontrolled comparisons support. The discipline has to reach the headline. Drop: Precision as decoration: four decimals with no method.

**Relationships.** The Counted Word: the count is a receipt; The Instrument: marks in the UI; The Specimen: stated boundaries.

### 9. The Witness — Emerging

**Idea.** The supplied robot as the figure that shows where you stand: at the eye point of the anamorphic room, on the stair, in the parade. A witness at signature scale, not a mascot that talks.

**Visual DNA.** The supplied robot (brain under glass, ear cups); Signature scale (never the headline); Placed at the meaningful point (E, the crossing, the crown); A small population (robots, dogs, a cat, a chicken).

**Formula.**
1. One robot per composition.
2. Put it where the viewer’s eye would be.
3. It acts; it does not explain.

**Evidence.** W18 Robot parade; W25 Mir, from one place · V06; W16 Reflection Identity · Reflection Walk; W10 Reflection brand kit · restored explorations; W21 Said and Kept · Microsite.

**Possibilities.** Motion idents and loaders; Empty states; Host of Field Notes; Stickers and merch.

**Critique.** Strong: Charming and specific; the parade proves the character can move. Develop: Three figures are in use (the supplied robot, the kit’s illustrated version, a red walker). D-004 still has “robot kept vs discarded” open. Drop: Talking-mascot copy. A new character beside the supplied one.

**Relationships.** One Object, Two Views: gives the stair a scale; The Specimen: competes with the moth; Worlds: can live in every world.

## 6. Techniques

Grouped by kind. Each names where it lives so it can be reused without re-deriving it.

**Formal**
- Counted digit cell: digits 1–9 in a fixed cell; tone from how many digits print, never from a gradient. W26 (blue field), W06, O07.
- Field-guide plate: numbered callouts on leader lines, scale bars, serif italic captions, a written line where the metaphor stops. W05, W05a, W07.
- Proof card: black ground, one signal color, the denominator kept beside every number. W11, W03, W29 (μ set as monuments).
- Witness placement: the supplied robot at signature scale, at the eye point of the composition. W18, W25.

**Computational**
- Z₃ residue codec (`scanZ3`): a–z → 1–26, digits kept, digital root dr(n) = 1 + ((n − 1) mod 9), channel by root mod 3. Verified: “Pick up where you left off.” → 21 counted, channels 3 · 7 · 11, matching the Said and Kept R3 chips.
- Helix projection (`createProjection`): θ = 2πk/42, r = r₀ + 4.2 · gain · ‖f − μ‖, y = 0.12k; tilt 0° → 90° rotates the same points from mark to wave.
- OKLab equal-lightness channels: Sky #5CC8FF, Lavender #B692FF, Cobalt #5B82FF normalized to L 0.74 so no channel reads as more important.
- T-04 Stock and light: a paper-tooth height field, one sheet light, a Beer–Lambert ink film; twelve effects from stipple to laser, worker-safe. Run unmodified in the exhibition.
- Gradient by Number: a light falloff quantized into digits per cell inside a glyph; the light can be dragged.
- Simulated record (W-landing `core.js`): seeded hash streams that stand in for a real record, labeled as simulated.

**Conceptual**
- One object, two views: a mark that is a projection, so it cannot be redrawn by hand.
- River and riverbed: models move, the record stays; the swap is an event in time, not a menu.
- Receipts: every claim carries a number, a unit, a population and a source.
- Worlds: a skin changes what Mindgut helps you find, not only how it looks.

**Systems**
- One record drives every surface: Codex’s worlds share one authored record; W-landing keeps its own (the baseline divergence in §4).
- Status vocabulary: measured · reported · simulated · proposed (receipts), held · parked · offered · struck (instrument marks).
- Recall: chords drawn 1 + 3 + 3 per turn in the 2.2.58 spec versus voluntary, hidden recall in “What Mir Is” (D-006, open).

## 9. Critique of the week

- **What worked.** The mechanism became the identity in three independent hands (W-landing V01, Reflection Identity, Codex’s worlds). That convergence is the best evidence the idea is real.
- **What diluted it.** A new metaphor per piece (moth, river, island, foldkeeper, worlds) and a new ground per piece (nine in eight days). Each was well made; together they read as five brands.
- **What overreached.** Case studies [INT] report results that were not controlled; the reliability pilot (W04) itself says no winner can be claimed. The Receipts territory exists to stop this.
- **Why work got lost.** Outputs landed in at least five places (Mindgut root, `Claude/`, the Ø project, Downloads, claude.ai artifacts), and the Field Guide exists as four byte-identical files under three names. One collection, one log entry per landing, and hashes on every copy would have kept the brand kit PDF from sitting alone in Downloads.
- **What to decide first.** D-004 (palette, type, robot) and the baseline μ. Every surface built after that inherits the answer.
