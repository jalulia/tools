Plate 2 / Four reasons

One record. Any model. No words shown.

Mindgut separates the model from the memory. The model is the voice in the room. The record is the part you own.

One record, any model.

Switch models mid-thought. The new one reads the earlier turns from the same record, and nothing below the handoff moves.

A suggestion stays a suggestion.

Every item is held, parked, offered or struck. What the model proposed never passes for what you decided.

It stays light.

Only four to seven exchanges are in focus at once. The rest passes into the record, so a long project does not mean a long window.

Yours, and wordless.

The record starts at zero, so every crossing in it is yours. Its picture is made of counts and positions. It never shows what was said.

Review of the current page

  

    
Keep
    
      
  • Claims discipline: every figure says simulated, every proposal says proposed, the form says it sends nothing.
    • 
      
  • The voice rule that a reading states an address, a count or a distance.
    • 
      
  • The method section that refuses to claim meaning for letter arithmetic.
    • 
      
  • The wordmark rule: supplied artwork, solid, never turned into data.
    • 
    
All four are carried into A, B and C unchanged.

    
Change
    
      
  • The arrival pins the page for about 7.6 screens before the four reasons appear. A first-time reader waits a long time for the reason to care. All three redesigns put the claim, the input and a finished figure in the first screen.
    • 
      
  • One material vocabulary (green halftone disc, isometric steps) carries every section, so sections are hard to tell apart at a glance.
    • 
      
  • Small labels in the third ink (#929a8d on #eef2e7) measure 2.56:1, below the 4.5:1 AA threshold for small text. Text inks in the redesigns measure 4.4:1 or higher.
    • 
      
  • The three audiences (personal desktop, studio pilot, research and builders) only appear as form buttons. Each redesign is aimed at one.
    • 
      
  • The supplied mascot sketches sit one section away from a voice rule that forbids emotional claims. See the mascot finding below.
    • 
    

  






  
Consumer research

  
Desk research only, English-language and mostly US sources, searched 11 Nov 2026. No interviews or tests were run, and non-English markets were not checked. Treat the implications as hypotheses to test, not findings about Mindgut's own users.

  

    
50%
More concerned than excited
Half of US adults say AI's growing role in daily life makes them more concerned than excited; 10% are more excited. About half or more say they have little or no control over how AI is used in their lives, and more than half want more. Pew Research Center, Mar 2026
For Mindgut: lead with ownership and visibility. "On your machine" and "a record you can see" answer the stated worry directly.

    
20
Users surprised by what was remembered
In a CHI 2026 interview study of 20 ChatGPT users, most had a negative reaction on learning what the system had remembered about them, and asked for more visibility, access and control over memory. Small qualitative sample. Chen, Molina, Liao and Cho, CHI '26
For Mindgut: the wordless picture is the differentiator. A record that shows counts and positions, never text, is memory without the surprise.

    
64%
Use two or more AI tools
A survey of 1,000 US adults found 42% use two AI tools and 22% use three or more. The survey was commissioned by a vendor that sells multi-model access, so read it as directional. Separately, app data shows ChatGPT's share of assistant use fell below 50% for the first time in May 2026, with users moving between assistants. Lorka / OnePoll, Jun 2026 / Sensor Tower via TechCrunch
For Mindgut: "Every AI you use is an island" describes a common situation now. The handoff demo belongs near the top, not in beat six.

    
3
Big-tech mascots in two months
Fast Company reports three large AI companies launched cute agent characters in Aug and Sep 2026, and argues the look is converging, invites fatigue, and may make people more forgiving of errors. Fast Company, Oct 2026
For Mindgut: none of the three redesigns uses a face. The kit's mascot sketches conflict with the brand's own voice rules; that call is yours and Mir's.

  






  
Design research

  

    
The category looks alike
Trade press through 2026 describes AI brands as visually interchangeable and reports a turn toward texture and tactile work in response (The Drum, Aug 2026; Creative Bloq). These are opinion pieces, not measurements. The memory-layer products found in search (developer-facing APIs and plug-ins) present as infrastructure. No consumer-facing memory product with a distinct visual identity turned up, which is absence of evidence only.

    
What your references ask for
T-04 sets one rule above the others: every effect reads the stock's height and is lit by one sheet light, and no gradient stands in for a material. Its Mindgut concepts draw readings as counted marks on real stock. The board splits into three families, and each redesign takes one: blue stipple and grain, screens and pixels, flat-color blocks with a black line.

  

  
Lineage, with caveats

  
    
    
  • Stipple. Tone built from counted dots runs through engraving, newspaper halftone and frequency-modulated screening; T-04 cites a void-and-cluster rank tile. Counting one dot per character borrows from dot-density maps, where a dot stands for a fixed quantity.
    • 
    
  • Weave. Weaving drafts are a notation that records a cloth's structure without picturing its pattern, close to what Mindgut claims for the record. The Jacquard loom is often placed in the ancestry of computing; historians differ on how direct that line is, so the page does not lean on it.
    • 
    
  • Brush. Flat inks with a keyline come from relief and screen printing, and block towers from construction toys. The risk named above applies: toy forms can make a system feel safer than its evidence supports, so C carries the same disclaimers as the others.
    •

Checked
    
      
  • Each direction rendered and reviewed at 1440 and 390 px wide, section by section, over several fix passes.
    • 
      
  • Record arithmetic lifted from the current page: scanner, center, reach, the 4 to 7 window, recall chords, six specimens.
    • 
      
  • Typing a line, model swap, advance, play, specimen loading and pointing work in all three.
    • 
      
  • Body and label text contrast at 4.4:1 or higher in each palette.
    • 
    

    
Open, for you and Mir
    
      
  • T-04's Mindgut concepts use the product's coset colors (147 blue, 258 purple). None of the three uses purple for the model. Say if the model's color is fixed.
    • 
      
  • The hero figure is now a 420-strand record with only the first strand yours. The current page uses 48.
    • 
      
  • The supplied illustrated layer and early boards are not carried over. Say if they should return.
    • 
      
  • Fonts are Google Fonts stand-ins. T-04's own display grotesque is not on that host.
