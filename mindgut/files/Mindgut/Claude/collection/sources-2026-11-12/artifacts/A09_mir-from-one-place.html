Mir, from one place

From E, a Fano plane and an ∞ of three rays read as one flat drawing in a mint gridded room. That is the only illusion. Everything else is sound: a stair to a door, a post-and-lintel frame, a gate. Every floating thing is held by tie rods laid along its own sight line, hidden from E and plain from anywhere else. The Mindgut robot stands at E, eyes on the point.

Trompe-l'œil audit
Coincidences that are not contacts
T a corner lands on another thing's edge. C two edges at different depths line up. K a sphere or the ∞ grazes an edge. Real contacts are not counted. Defaults: 0 at both holds.

  






  

    
Soundness

      
Stair. One solid, risers 0.18 m, going 0.27 m: 2R + G = 0.63 m (Blondel). It lands at a door in the wall, under a guard 1.0 m high.

      
Frame. Plinth, shaft, capital; the beam bears on both capitals, its center between them.

      
Floating things. Seven spheres and the ∞ hang on rods that run back along their sight lines to stone or wall. From E a rod sits behind what it holds.

      
Checked at every build: each part rests on the floor, on another part or a wall; no two parts overlap; the stair rule; the guard height; the rods hidden from E.
