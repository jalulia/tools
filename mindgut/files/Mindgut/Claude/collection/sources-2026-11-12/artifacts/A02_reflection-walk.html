Every message is a step.

The record climbs once around every 42 messages. Green steps are yours, pale steps are the model's. A step reaches out as far as its message departs from the record's own center. The lit steps at the top are what the model is reading now. Beside it is the same record seen down its axis. That view is the Reflection from the kit: every message is one lobe, and the two fine lines are you and the model. The app calls a step a strand.

Stand upMemory
Stand up: share of crossings further from the center than a random English strand of the same length. Memory: lag-1 correlation of that distance inside a session. A simulated record has almost none, by construction.

Order is the climb

Nothing is rearranged. Message 1 is the bottom step and the newest is the top one, 42 to a full turn, so the same position on the dial always means the same point in a turn.

Reach is departure

Each message is counted into three letter channels. A step is as long as that mix sits from the record's own center. Short steps are ordinary. Long ones are where something changed.

Lit is in focus

The window holds 4 to 7 exchanges. On the eighth it passes the oldest three into the spine, which pales with age and does not move again.

Violet is recalled

From the newest step, seven earlier ones are pulled back in: the first, three of yours, three of the model's. The rule drawn here is a proposal. Point at any step for its counts.

    Method / What is measuredScanner math from Mir

    Three channels. No meanings.

    A line of type goes in. Three counts come out. Type below to run it. The page states what the arithmetic does and claims nothing past it.

    Why reach is measured against chance

    A short message swings wide whatever it says, because a handful of letters cannot split evenly three ways. The horn is the swing that chance alone gives at each length. A message inside it is ordinary. A message outside it is standing out, and only those reach far on the stair.

    Why the center moves

    Ordinary English is not even across the channels. From standard letter frequencies it sits near (0.28, 0.39, 0.32). Plotted against one third each, every record would show the same lean, which is English and not the record. So the axis of the horn is the record's own mean. Seen end-on, the horn is the channel plane.

    What the names are

    Assertion, Relation and Derivation are Mindgut's structural reading of the three channels. They are names for residues. They are not a claim that letter arithmetic understands what a sentence means.

    The arithmetic, in full

    Every record here is simulated: each message is a seeded draw of characters into the three channels at rates near the English baseline. No conversation was read to make this page, and none is shown.

    Yours to keep.

    No two reflections alike.

    It grows, it doesn't score.

    Geist at 300, 400 and 500, with Geist Mono for labels and figures. Nothing typeset is heavier than 500, so the wordmark is always the heaviest mark in view. A headline turns its last word or two to forest and closes with a period. "Say it once. It stays." waits on a defined retention and deletion policy.

    Mir remembers everything about you.Mindgut keeps a record you can see.
    She understands how you feel.It draws from the record.

    Here is what you said on Tuesday.Crossing 412, turn 9.8, Carbon.

    English, sentence case, present tense, "you" for the reader, "it" for Mir. No emotional or consciousness claims. A reading states an address, a count or a distance.
