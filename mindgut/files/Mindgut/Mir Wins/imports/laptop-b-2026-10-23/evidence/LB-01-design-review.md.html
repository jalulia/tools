# Open Studio review

The white interface is the primary daily surface. Broad colour and material expression sit outside sustained reading and source inspection. Emerald remains the signature; teal is a signal, vermilion an object colour, blue an editorial register and yellow a moment of humour.

The original wordmark and robot are retained. Overprinted source sheets, letter fields and displaced engraving films establish a shared material vocabulary around words and time. All geometry is an illustration, never presented as measured PFD output.

The conversation, HELIX inspector, source dialog and visible history use fictional examples. Persistent storage, retrieval, model changes and sharing are interaction proposals; no live model calls or actual account actions occur. Exports are labeled fictional.

Review changes: restored contrast to old records; fixed mobile overflow and hero breaks; completed the booklet interior; replaced decorative concentric light with layered engraved film; kept motion user-controlled and reduced-motion compatible.

The deliverable is self-contained HTML. The canonical PDF remains local and is not bundled. Source-grounding notes and automated checks are retained beside the build.

## Typography refinement
Removed the condensed display family and serif accent. Archivo now carries display, reading and UI; mono is retained for date/source notation. Resized and respaced hero, printed sheets, posters, booklet and closing for normal-width letterforms. Removed italic switches from editorial headings. Desktop campaign and hero views reviewed, mobile booklet and 390px horizontal fit checked. No behavior changed.
