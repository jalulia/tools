Grounding & scope

Product grounding: What Mir Is, Chris Sojka, September 22, 2026. §2 (pp. 2–3): human, model and Mindgut. §3 (pp. 3–4): main path without a Mir-prescribing system prompt, HELIX, recent turns, voluntary recall. §4 (p. 5): changing models. §5 (p. 6): interruptions. §8 (p. 8): regeneration from accumulated conditions.

Visual starting point: Antare’s early explorations in the supplied Mindgut moodboard: open halo, floor of light, presence at the edge of a quiet reading surface. This direction reinterprets those forms in emerald and neon teal.

All conversations are fictional. Activity states, context selection and spatial browsing are design proposals. Light, brightness and geometry are not measurements of emotion, awareness, accuracy or memory quality. The prototype stores no entries beyond the current page session.
