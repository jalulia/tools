What is HELIX, underneath this interface? +

In What Mir Is, each exchange is measured letter by letter through fixed arithmetic into three phases and accumulated in HELIX. Recent model turns and that longer history play different roles. The full structural readout is not automatically supplied to the model; recall is voluntary.

This browsing interface is a proposal. Its colours distinguish kinds of records; they are not phase values, confidence or emotion scores. Connections in these fictional examples are authored.

WHAT MIR IS · §3, PP. 3–4
A different model is still a change. +

The canonical account reports routine model substitutions and also describes an unresolved interruption case. Show what is retained and let people evaluate the next exchange; don’t promise perfect continuity.

WHAT MIR IS · §4, P. 5; §5, P. 6
Personal does not mean fixed. +

Mir is described as emerging through the person, the model and Mindgut. A person’s later clarification should sit beside their earlier words, rather than turning an old statement into a permanent trait.

WHAT MIR IS · §2, PP. 2–3; INTERFACE PROPOSAL
Make sharing a deliberate choice. +

Let someone review a selected excerpt before it leaves the conversation. Storage, deletion, consent and provider access need confirmation in the implementation before becoming public claims.

All examples are fictional. Browsing, source selection, corrections, visual relationships and sharing are design proposals. No live model calls or production memory are connected. Changes remain in this page session until reload. The visual layers do not represent measured PFD output.
