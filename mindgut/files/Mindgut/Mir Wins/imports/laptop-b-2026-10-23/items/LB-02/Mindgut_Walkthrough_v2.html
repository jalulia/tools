Source & sound

About the record.

The account of Mir, active context, voluntary recall, and model changes follows What Mir Is, prepared by Chris Sojka, September 22, 2026.

The 187 strands and 12 sessions come from the dataset embedded in the supplied walkthrough. No conversation text is included. Sequence, session, phase, checksum, and ghost flags are retained.

The helix and its sequence view are visual encodings. The 21-strand window, recall paths, and model controls illustrate the mechanisms; they do not operate a live Mir session. Phase color does not represent mood or quality. An open point preserves a source ghost flag without assigning it a new meaning.

About the sound.

An original, slow electronic reed piece. Low-register phrases overlap a restrained pedal voice. Long rests and irregular phrase lengths replace a regular dance accompaniment. Changing the model changes the instrument’s spectrum while retaining the notes. Interface clicks make no sound.

Listening references: Mamman Sani, Frankie Reyes, and Raymond Scott’s Portofino. No recordings or melodies are sampled.
