## 1. What the actual components communicate

The workspace source is the 2.2.59 product DOM with one synthetic session: a user turn with `notes.txt`, a Mir turn ending in `project-plan.md`, a three-item To Do at 2/3, Output/Drafts/Input lists, and a sidebar whose values are placeholders (6 calls, 12,840 in / 2,160 out, 6 exchanges, HELIX 248 — "187 founding · 61 organic"). Every handler in `preview-source.js` is local: send, enter, attach, mic and `[data-example-only]` controls all route to a feedback line ("Example composer — messages are not sent"). So the specimen is honest by construction, and the review is about what the real DOM says when shown, not what it does.

**Three things the components communicate, in order of how much they matter:**

**(a) The conversation and the record are two different panels.** The chat column holds turns, attachments and outputs; the sidebar holds the instrument: Tokens, Respiration ("6 exchanges · next renewal at 21"), the Ø tension count, HELIX with its strand count, the Automator, Scanner, To Do and the three file lists. The DOM makes the separation Gaby's brief asks the statement to explain — model turns on the left, the measured history on the right — a visible fact, not a claim. This is the single most useful thing the specimen can show and it needs no annotation beyond section labels.

**(b) Normal mode hides the instrument; Advanced mode shows it.** `[data-mode="normal"] .engine-only { display: none }` applies to Tokens (second block), Heat, Respiration, Ø and HELIX, and to `.msg-phase`, `.msg-status`, `.msg-time` and `.msg-attempts` on each message. In Normal mode a reader sees a chat with a To Do and files; in Advanced they see the measurement. The hub should show both states of the same DOM side by side, because the *toggle itself* is the product's answer to "how much of this do I need to know?" — and because showing only Advanced would misstate how a person normally sits with it.

**(c) The session is bounded and says so.** Respiration shows "6 exchanges" against a 4·5·6·7 scale and "next renewal at 21"; the composer can be queued and stopped (`#sendBtn.queued` → "Example stopped. No model is running."); the status dot has `connected` and `working` states. These are small, but together they communicate that a session has a shape and a ceiling — the honest counterpart to any sentence about "accumulating" context. The specimen should keep the Respiration block visible in at least one state rather than cropping to the chat.

**The one misrepresentation to avoid above all others:** presenting the sidebar's measurement as a readout of *this conversation*. The values are synthetic (the file says so: "All example values are synthetic"), the HELIX count "187 founding · 61 organic" is not Julia's record, and the Thinkfeeling block is explicitly "Illustrative collapsed reasoning area. Actual session content is not included." If the hub crops the specimen so a reader believes the numbers describe the exchange they are reading, it has turned a component specimen into fake telemetry — the exact thing Julia's note names ("made up interface and functionality"). Label once, at the specimen's edge, in the product's own `.sp-label` style: *Product interface, v2.2.59 · example session · values are placeholders.* Then never again on the page.

**Four smaller cautions, from the source:**

- **Colour.** The product's Normal-mode tokens are `--brand-mint #2ac9a0`, `--brand-cool #22a884`, `--brand-warm #3ddb85` — all mint-green, which Julia has excluded. Light theme defines `--c258: #9933cc` (purple) and `--c147: #0088cc` (blue). A violet/teal refinement therefore has to be a **deliberate re-skin of the Normal-mode accent variables**, and the hub should say the accent was refined for the study; it must not imply the product ships violet. The Advanced-mode coset colours (`--c147/--c258/--c369`) carry meaning in the product and should not be remapped to taste.
- **Type.** The product's `--sans`, `--mono` and `--display` all resolve to `'Roboto', 'Helvetica Neue', Arial`. Zero `condensed` or `font-stretch` in the CSS. Archivo is the hub's face, not the product's; keep the specimen in the product's stack or state the substitution.
- **Mode naming.** The toggle's aria-label is "Toggle normal/advanced mode" and the view ids are `normal` / `advanced`. Use those words. Don't rename them for the hub.
- **"Founding · organic."** The HELIX line reads "187 founding · 61 organic". I don't have a documented definition of those two terms in the source I read; don't gloss them in a caption unless the spec defines them.
