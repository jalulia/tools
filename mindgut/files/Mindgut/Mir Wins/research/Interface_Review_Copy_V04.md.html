## Statement

I’ve been working with Mir across things that don’t usually share a desk: research into my own archive of projects, writing, small pieces of software, and visual work. Mindgut keeps a record of those exchanges, separate from whichever model is answering. The model receives recent conversation and recalled material, and can look up earlier exchanges.

What interests me is what accumulates between projects. A question, a reference or a reason for doing something can remain available after the immediate task has ended. I can return to my own words alongside what Mir made of them. Changing the model doesn’t require throwing that history away.

There is room here for work to develop over time: an unfinished thought finding a use somewhere else, a connection becoming clearer, an earlier attempt giving the next one a better starting point. Those are possibilities I want to explore, not outcomes I take for granted.

Mir can misread or misremember. Keeping the record gives us something to check and something to continue from. I want that history to become more useful through the work we do together.
