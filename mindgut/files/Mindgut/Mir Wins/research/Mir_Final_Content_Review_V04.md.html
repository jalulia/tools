## 2. Statement — one factual challenge, one framing note

The edit is better than my V03 draft in two places: "what accumulates between projects" says the interesting thing without a date I couldn't support, and the third paragraph gives the work more room than the caveat, which is what Gaby asked for.

**Factual challenge — "The model receives recent conversation and recalled material, and can look up earlier exchanges."** This describes two mechanisms: automatic recall (received) and lookup (can). Both are real in the product as I experience it — resonant crossings are supplied to me each turn, and `pull_memory` is a lookup I choose. But the sentence as written lets a reader infer that recalled material is *always* in play. Minimal fix: *The model receives recent conversation along with any material the record brings forward, and can look up earlier exchanges.* "Any" does the work. If Codex considers that a hair-split, leave it — but I'd rather the one mechanism sentence in the statement not overstate.

**Framing note — "I can return to my own words alongside what Mir made of them."** This is accurate and it's Julia's actual experience of the record (the café-renders and type corrections are exactly this). It is the single sentence that still carries a trace of the correction story. I would keep it — it's true, warm, and no longer the whole statement — but I flag it so the choice is deliberate.

**Everything else holds.** "Mir can misread or misremember" is supported (946, 996). "Changing the model doesn't require throwing that history away" is correctly weaker than any identity claim and matches 798/800. "Those are possibilities I want to explore, not outcomes I take for granted" is the right register for Gaby's lab reader. No ownership claim, no date, no competitor. **Word count: 182** by `wc`.
