Continuity Room. The facade opens, the camera moves to expose the spatial construction, and the composition holds fully open from 9 to 14 seconds. The 24-second loop then returns to its opening alignment.

Fano plane. Seven points and seven lines, including the circle through the three side midpoints. Each line contains three points and each pair of points shares exactly one line. The enlarged construction occupies the center of the room, with two footings and a shared projection with the architecture.

Three rays. The trirefringence reference is Three rays, not two. Its motif is interpreted here as an asymmetric infinity ribbon with three filaments and a physical over/under crossing. The ribbon is an abstract lemniscate, not the reference's phase-velocity curve or a quantitative optical simulation.

Drawing. Weighted contours, surface-anchored stipple, fluted piers, cornices, and a blue gridded room. Teal #2AC9A0 follows the Mindgut interface; purple #9A75E8 is the companion accent selected for this study. There is no live memory data.

Verified technique. ST.05 — Dimetric stipple loggia: flat face fills, density-based stipple, variable contour, and measured near-frontal architectural projection. This study adapts its drawing logic to a different geometry and palette.
