What this is. What it isn’t.

A standalone interface exploration informed by Mindgut Boards V05, Skin Worlds V08, and the front-end studies library. No production landing-page source was supplied or modified. All artwork is procedural; no runtime downloads, external fonts, or packages.

Frequency translates the boards’ operated instrument, counted record, and phosphor-log references into a sculptural signal desk. Afterimage makes the record an optical field and the conversation a crisp, inset surface. Redline develops print registration through oversized sans typography and three-channel impressions. No cream. No serif type.

Mir responses are scripted and labeled. Existing sample messages about attachments and Output are sample dialogue, not working integrations. Recall uses Euclidean distance between letter-count proportions; it does not interpret meaning. Refresh clears the demo. Transcript export includes only this local demonstration.

Keyboard: arrow keys/Home/End choose worlds, numbered buttons choose records, Ctrl/Command+Enter sends a line. Motion honors reduced-motion preferences. Canvas interactions also have button alternatives.
