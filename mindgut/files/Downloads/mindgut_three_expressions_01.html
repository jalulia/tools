A · Instrument

The app’s palette taken as given. Sage ground, chartreuse as the only UI accent, Julia’s forest green as the one second value for the hero core and text-weight accents. The cheapest path to “one identity”: nothing on the site that isn’t already in the app.

B · Field notes

Julia’s docs tokens (white, wash, ink, forest) with one warm ember borrowed from our gold/red. Reads as a notebook. Closest to the surface the team has already been living in for a month; the ember is the only thing added.

C · Standing wave

Our full gold / red / violet / blue field kept inside the hero, on the app’s sage ground. Everything outside the hero is the app. The most expressive of the three and the only one that keeps the four-colour field; the cost is two accents on the page instead of one.

Same build in all three. The rows are the only things that differ, plus one honest read on each.
