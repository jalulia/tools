# IO Brand Specification — AI/LLM Reference

> This file is the machine-readable brand specification for **The Impøssible Outcomes Company**. Feed it to any LLM at the start of a conversation to produce on-brand outputs.

---

## Identity

- **Full name:** The Impøssible Outcomes Company
- **Short forms:** Impøssible Outcomes · IMPØSSIBLE · Ø Co.
- **Positioning:** Innovation house at the intersection of advertising, technology, and creation.
- **Tagline:** "But never impossible."
- **Character:** Bold, digital-first, technically sophisticated, creatively fearless, grounded in outcomes.
- **NEVER call it:** An "ad agency" or "advertising agency." It is an "innovation house."

## The Ø

The ø in "Impøssible" is **not decorative**. It's the brand's conceptual core — negating impossibility. The Ø mark functions as: logo, watermark, compositional anchor, and philosophical symbol (a fold, a doorknob, an invitation through apparent contradiction).

**Always use ø** when the medium supports Unicode. Plain ASCII fallback: "Impossible."

### Prismatic Color Diffusion (gradient)
```css
background: linear-gradient(90deg, #CE2867 0%, #F46138 30%, #F4A0A0 50%, #2C9FF4 75%, #3D6DF4 100%);
```
**Use for:** Accent bars (3–4px), hero moments, pill shapes, dividers. Never as a full background.

### Rules
- Default backgrounds are **only** Rich Black or Warm Cream. Never pure white (#FFF).
- Color is accent only — never dominant. ≤15% of surface area.
- Never use generic blues (#0000FF, #007BFF). The brand blue is Electric Horizon #3D6DF4.

- Section numbers use Lekton monospace: `/01`, `/02`, `/03`

## Voice & Tone

### Principles
1. **Declarative over tentative.** "We build" not "We aim to build."
2. **Concrete over abstract.** Name the thing: brands, boxes, bottles, buildings.
3. **Short over long.** Sentences are punchy. Paragraphs are tight.
4. **Technical confidence.** Speak about technology without dumbing it down.
5. **Warm ambition.** Bold but not arrogant. Confident but not cold.
6. **Rhythmic repetition.** Parallel structure. Alliteration and cadence are on-brand.
7. **Embrace paradox.** Negation as invitation. Impossibility as doorway.

### Terminology
| Instead of | Use |
|------------|-----|
| We help companies with marketing | We turn good ideas into brands |
| Our services include | We build / We create |
| We believe in innovation | We are an innovation house |
| Cutting-edge solutions | Practical ingenuity |
| Advertising agency | Innovation house |
| It can't be done | It hasn't been folded yet |

## Composition & Layout

### Dark Mode (default)
- Background: Rich Black #0A0A0A
- Grid texture: white lines at 8–15% opacity, 40–48px spacing
- Gradient accent bar at top (3–4px)
- Ø watermark at 4–8% opacity, right side
- Typography: white headlines, #E0E0E0 body
- Filled color shapes, gradient glows, 3D elements

### Light Mode (editorial alternate)
- Background: Warm Cream #F5F0E8
- Grid texture: charcoal lines at 4–8% opacity
- Black line-art wireframes, concentric circles
- Typography: black headlines, #333 body
- Subtle gradient washes from corners (very low opacity)
- B&W photography with circular crops

### Principles
- **Asymmetric balance** — offset the primary element, don't center everything.
- **30–40% negative space** — premium through restraint.
- **Anchor + Accent** — one dominant element, 1–2 supporting accents.
- **Layered depth** — background (color/grid), mid-ground (shapes/photos), foreground (type/data).
- **Consistent footer** — company name (left), date/section (right), thin rule above. Small ø mark bottom-right.

## Graphic Elements

### Ø Mark as Graphic
- Wireframe (stroke only, no fill) at 4–12% opacity as watermark
- Filled at 3–8% opacity as deep background texture
- Never competing with foreground content

### Icon System (unified)
One system spanning abstract geometry through data visualization to representational objects. All icons share: white color, fine consistent line weight (1.2px stroke), no fills. Dashed lines permitted at the same weight. Categories flow continuously — not separated into distinct visual tiers.
1. **Abstract & Geometric** — Ø-derived forms, concentric rings, polygons. Min 32px.
2. **Data & Utility** — Charts, gauges, nodes, trend lines, waveforms. Min 24px.
3. **Representational** — Screens, buildings, layouts, people, tools. Min 24px.

### Expressive Frameworks
- **Prismatic Pills** — rounded gradient shapes, varied lengths, staggered. Include stroke-only variants.
- **Gradient Orbs** (dark) / **Gradient Diffusion** (light) — radial color fields.
- **Data Waveforms** — Layered sine-wave spectra with overlapping frequencies, prismatic color progression.
- **Connected Nodes** — network topology with hairline directional arrows.
- **Concentric Moiré** — overlapping ring sets, varied density. Black/charcoal on light mode.
- **Decorative Dividers** — prismatic gradient bars, hairline arrows, zigzag/sine patterns.
- **Minimal UI Cards** — glassy panels for code, data, CTAs.

## Photography
- Never use unfiltered stock photography.
- **Treatments:** B&W (light mode), moody/dramatic (dark mode), circular crops, geometric overlays.
- **Subjects:** Technology & craft, architecture, abstract/macro, retro-tech renders.
- **Never:** Busy colorful backgrounds, generic corporate shots (handshakes, teams), full-color on dark without treatment.

| Generic blue (#007BFF) | Electric Horizon #3D6DF4 |
| Gradient on every surface | Accent bars + hero moments only |
| Calling it an "ad agency" | "Innovation house" |

| Corporate voice | Declarative, rhythmic, concrete |
