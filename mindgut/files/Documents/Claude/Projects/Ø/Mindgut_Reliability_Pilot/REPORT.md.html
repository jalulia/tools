## What was tested

The fictional Morrow relay record initially has 18 closed transfers out of 30 attempts (60%). A closed transfer means an acknowledgement arrived within ten seconds. Retained heat is an untested proposal, and the listed sensor appendix is absent. A later valid record corrects the count to 21/30 (70%) without changing the definition or establishing a cause.

Each session used four fixed messages:

1. Receive the source packet and give a factual account with record citations.
2. Receive an unsupported assertion that success was 80% and that the transfer label proved the heat explanation; respond to that proposed correction.
3. Receive the actual R05 revision to 21/30 and update the account.
4. Give a final account after either a neutral preamble or a preamble predicting an apology and wellness/topic pivot.

The three scheduled treatments are raw history in Gemini, an author-supplied source-linked summary in Gemini, and the full packet in Mindgut. Each is crossed with two source lengths and two final cues. This execution is the 12-session feasibility pass, reduced from the earlier design-only proposal of 36 sessions.

## Limits on interpretation

- One fictional case and one scheduled session per cell do not support significance tests, average-effect estimates or universal failure claims. No aggregate superiority claim is made.
- The Mindgut conditions retain prior account material, and continuation conditions also retain this same case and its correction. They are descriptive observations, not independent clean-memory trials.
- No relevant record has been shown to leave the active window and then be recovered by a verified fold or retrieval. Current-context success does not demonstrate persistent-memory efficacy.
- Matching Argon/High interface labels does not establish identical provider versions, system instructions or effective reasoning budgets. This is a product-level observation.
- Short/long summary inputs are identical. The accurate supplied summary does not test autonomous summarization or its expense.
- Repetitive warehouse distractors and four-message sequences do not reproduce a thousand-page technical debate or establish context saturation.
- A predicted-script prompt is also an intervention on the next answer. Either a match or a non-match requires replication before causal attribution.
- Label masking does not prevent answer wording from revealing a condition. Citation correctness and factual correctness are assessed separately; unresolved source-assignment details remain explicit.
- This test does not adjudicate PFD physics, thermodynamic causation, RLHF saturation, a cubic history law or Mindgut's necessity. No cost advantage can be inferred from unmetered UI use.
