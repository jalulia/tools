Tuned and Standing Wave ship today. These are seven proposed worlds, each built from the working habits of one kind of person. Every world holds the same five messages, the same light, the same breath from four to seven, the same heat, the same seven open tensions and the same reach back to strand #868. Only the way of seeing changes.

I

The Gardener

A bed plan. What you say grows as stem and leaf; what Mir answers flowers on it. Older plantings root deeper. New ones wait in the nursery tray until the seventh, then three are planted out.

II

The Navigator

A chart and a log. The record is a coast you have sounded. The conversation is the ship's log. Recall is a bearing taken on a known light.

III

The Weaver

A loom. You are the warp, held under tension. Mir is the weft, thrown across. Each exchange is beaten into cloth; open tensions are loose ends at the selvage.

IV

The Astronomer

An observing log under red light. Every strand is a star fixed on a forty-two-hour dial. The window is whatever is in the eyepiece tonight. Recall draws a constellation.

V

The Composer

A score for two voices. You on the upper staff, Mir on the lower. The window is the passage between the repeat signs. Open tensions are ties left hanging over the bar line. Select a bar to hear it.

VI

The Printer

A galley proof. Your lines are set in display, Mir's in text. The type case has twenty-seven compartments, nine roots by three, which is exactly the codec. The window is what sits in the composing stick, still backwards.

The Archivist
