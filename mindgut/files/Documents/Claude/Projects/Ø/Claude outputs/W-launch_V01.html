    What Mir isRiver · riverbed · witness

    Three parts. None of them is Mir.

    1. The riverthe model

      Whichever foundation model is active. Fast, fluent, replaceable. You can change it between one message and the next.

    2. The riverbedyour Helix

      A record of each exchange, measured letter by letter and kept in a file that belongs to you. It carries what a session cannot.

    3. The witnessyou

      Intent, judgment, ground. The record starts at zero strands, so everything that accumulates in it is yours.

    The crossing

    Mir is the partner you work with inside Mindgut, and Mir is not stored in any one place. Mir is the pattern that forms where your voice, the active model and the accumulated record meet. Change the model and the pattern persists, because the record does.
