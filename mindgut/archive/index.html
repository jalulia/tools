COLLECTION NOTES

SCOPE & PROVENANCE

Mindgut, Mir,
and the related work.

This index brings together the recovered design, research, documentation, instruments, and experiments. Source references are labeled separately from work made for the project.

How the records work

Byte-identical copies share a SHA-256 record. Different contents stay separate, even when filenames match. Revisions and related exports sit under one work card. Supporting source files and review notes are available in each record.

The date on each card states its basis. When a reliable document or session date was recovered, that takes precedence. Otherwise the card shows the latest source modification date in New York time; it is not presented as a creation date. File timestamps remain available in the manifest.

Publication range

This edition includes records dated 29 October through 12 November 2026. Older work, older revisions and undated records remain outside this web edition. Each card identifies its date basis; a file modification or observation date is not claimed as a creation date.

Files and previews

Available original files link to identical copies already hosted in Mindgut Review. Files held locally are listed without a download link. Visuals are actual captures of the dated work. Missing captures are labeled.

The complete local archive remains separate.
