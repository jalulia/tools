Subject A · Botanical

Sacred lotus after the woodblock manner: every petal is built on its own curved spine, so the vein fan follows the petal's twist rather than the page. Depth is carried by four tints held just at the threshold of visibility, and by rank — nothing is shaded.

FIG. 1 — NELUMBO NUCIFERA · OPEN FLOWER, BUD, PAD Bowl perspective: petal bases sit on an ellipse, each petal recurving on its own spine. Receptacle drawn as a foreshortened disc with seated carpels.

Construction

  • 01 Each petal is a spine plus a width profile; the outline is offset along the spine normal.
  • 02 Veins are the same spine at fractional offsets, converging at the base and fanning to the tip.
  • 03 Four tints separate rank; the deepest is barely two per cent from white.
  • 04 Tips recurve and some fold — a folded tip is a second contour, not a wiggle in the first.
FIG. 2 — SPINE, ENVELOPE, VENATION, FOLD The four stages behind every petal in FIG. 1, at 2×.
FIG. 3 — PELTATE PAD · RADIAL VENATION, SINUATE MARGIN Veins radiate from the petiole insertion and terminate in the margin's scallops; secondary veins branch only between primaries.
