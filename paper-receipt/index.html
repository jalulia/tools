Receipt · Front-end component

Paper receipt

The receipt from the POP work, lifted out as one component: DOM type on a monospace grid, a paper canvas, a cut edge and an SVG ink filter.

Three styles came out of it between 14 and 15 October: R2's clean slip, R3's sectioned small paper with starved ink and a stamp, and R4's one-face 80 mm thermal with a density field in the ink. Each is a preset, and each value it recorded is a parameter here; the default is R4 as tuned in this tool on 26 November. The Mindgut robot heads the slip in ASCII, set in the body face.

How it is built
  1. Layout. Rows in one monospace face. Rules and leaders are repeated characters clipped by overflow, so they always fill the measure; R2 and R3 draw them as 1 px borders instead.
  2. Paper. One canvas under the type at device resolution: a seeded tooth per pixel, slow row tone, optional head banding and strands. Nothing tiles.
  3. Edge. A clip-path polygon cut from the sheet: the tear bar's zigzag, a torn fbm edge, perforation notches or a straight cut.
  4. Starved ink. feDisplacementMap moves each glyph a fraction of a pixel; a hole mask, α = slope · noise + offset, knocks out what the head missed.
  5. Density field. A slow noise three times wider than tall, mixed with a fine one, multiplies the ink: α = depth · noise + floor.
  6. Falloff. The surface fades over the slip at top and bottom, three chained drop-shadows set it on the surface, and an optional mask thins the ink toward the end and the sides.
Style elements
The robot

Drawn from the Mindgut logomark (Mindgut/sandbox/brand/robot.png): the coiled brain under a glass dome, the rim, two ear bolts, round eyes, the angled nose, the open smile and the grille. Large head 41 × 18 characters; small head 21 × 9 for the foot. Both are plain ASCII and copy from Export.
