Flat-ink bouquet systems: spot colour. coverage grain. hand keyline.

Every plate is generated in the browser from code — SVG geometry for the drawing, canvas for paper and grain, SVG filters for ink physics. Nothing is a bitmap. One printing process runs under every plate: each colour is its own run through a coverage filter, one keyline pass gets a hand-waver and a misregistration shift, and the sheet finishes with grain veil + paper tooth. Change one seed and the whole sheet re-prints differently.

Framework lifted from the keyline & ink gallery. This first plate isolates one variable — the ink grammar — on a single bouquet bloom.
