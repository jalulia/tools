Keyline & Ink · component snippet gallery

Flat-ink botanical print systems: keyline, spot color, screen texture, hand-set display type.

Every plate below is generated in the browser from code — SVG geometry for the drawing, canvas for paper and grain, SVG filters for ink physics. Nothing is a bitmap. Open “source” under any plate to lift the builder function into your own project.

Study 01 is a faithful re-drawing of the riso lotus reference; 02–05 isolate one variable each (linework, texture & color, type, assembly); 06–08 are complete original pieces in three dialects of the same language.
