Quiet objects do not an­nounce them­selves. They wait in the corner of a room for the light to arrive, and when it does they hold it, briefly, the way a shelf holds a bowl. COLS 5–12 · BODY

The module is the argument. Column spans are declared, not improvised — a caption occupies four tracks, a figure occupies four, a running text stretches across eight. Set the rhythm once and the page composes itself.

Alignment stops being a decision and becomes a property of the system. Gutters are constant; the tracks flex. When a block wants to be wider it takes another track — never half of one. Edges land where the ruler says they land, and the reader feels the order before they can name it.

[ 001 ] — The studio

We make brands that get to work.

Ostmark is a small, opinionated practice building identity systems for companies that would rather be precise than loud. We start with structure — a typographic spine, a grid, a set of rules — and let the expression follow from there.

No mood-board theatrics, no decoration for its own sake. Every system we ship is documented, testable, and built to survive contact with a real team on a real deadline.
