[ 001 ] — The studio

We make brands that get to work.

Ostmark is a small, opinionated practice building identity systems for companies that would rather be precise than loud. We start with structure — a typographic spine, a grid, a set of rules — and let the expression follow from there.

No mood-board theatrics, no decoration for its own sake. Every system we ship is documented, testable, and built to survive contact with a real team on a real deadline.
