· ⁂ ·

Quiet objects do not an­nounce them­selves. They wait in the corner of a room for the light to arrive, and when it does they hold it, briefly, the way a shelf holds a bowl. We wanted an evening that behaves like this — un­hurried, low in the voice, gen­erous with si­lence — where each thing on the table is al­lowed its own weather, and no­body is asked to look for longer than they wish to.

— from the exhibition notes, aw26
